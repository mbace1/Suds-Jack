Ride up a quarter pipe fast to launch off the lip; too slow and you roll back down. Hold grind into the coping instead and the board stalls on it — the direction you hold names the stall — then ollie out or let it roll you back in. Ollie onto the funbox or a crate with grind held to ride its edge; come in across a line for a boardslide, along it for a 50-50. Chain tricks in the air, land straight to bank the combo. Crates and walls are solid, and landing sideways at speed is a bail.
