# Art sheet request: WORLD 7 — ICE MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31: "you can build all levels/worlds now"). Same
format as the World 3 and 4 requests and the approved World 2 sheet. Claude
built World 7 from this brief in v72; a sheet the owner approves can still
change it.

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 7 — ICE
MODULES". Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: the river frozen. The arena is a sheet of pale blue ice, 56 × 34 m,
cracked, air caught in it, frost creeping in from its edges, seen from above
at about 30°, in a snowfield at night. Skating blobs (SKATA) live here, and the
boss is BERG. The floor is slippery.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and lathes.
- The floor stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing stands along the near side.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Ice crystal cluster: three to six six-sided prisms coming to points, the
   tallest 1.6–3.6 m and near upright, the rest leaning out from one root.
2. Snowdrift: a long smooth mound, 1.4–2.6 m long, banked against a
   cluster's foot.
3. Snow-capped rock: a dark faceted rock, 0.8–2 m, snow on its top.
4. Snowfield: snow ribbed by the wind, snow blowing across it.
5. Ice floor: pale ice, darker where it is deep, hairline cracks.
6. Air in the ice: small rings of caught bubbles, 0.25–0.6 m.
7. Frosted edge: frost creeping in from the floor's edge.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the ice from above at 30°, crystal clusters and
   drifts along the far side and both sides, rocks between.
