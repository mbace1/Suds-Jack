# Art sheet request: WORLD 8 — DEEP MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31: "you can build all levels/worlds now"). Same
format as the World 3 and 4 requests and the approved World 2 sheet. Claude
built World 8 from this brief in v72; a sheet the owner approves can still
change it.

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 8 — DEEP
MODULES". Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: down to the sea floor, where no light reaches. The arena is a bed
of dark silt in slow ripples, 48 × 32 m, specks of light living in it, seen
from above at about 30°; it is lit only by what lives there. Glowing blobs
(GLOWA) live here, and the boss is ANGLER. The dark closes in and opens.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and lathes.
- The floor stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing stands along the near side.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Kelp: a clump of two to five ribbons, 1.8–4.6 m tall, twisting up their
   length and swaying, beads of light down their middles.
2. Boulder: a rounded rock, 1.4–3 m, barnacles on its top.
3. Anemone: a cluster of a dozen tentacles, 0.8–1.3 m, their tips glowing
   pink or blue, at a boulder's foot.
4. Vent: a small mound in the silt with bubbles rising out of it.
5. Bubble stream: a column of small bubbles rising and wobbling.
6. Seabed: dark silt in slow ripples, faintly blue.
7. Specks: the tiny lights living in the floor, twinkling.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the seabed from above at 30°, kelp, boulders and
   vents along the far side and both sides.
