# Art sheet request: WORLD 5 — BOILER MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31: "you can build all levels/worlds now"). Same
format as the World 3 and 4 requests and the approved World 2 sheet. Claude
built World 5 from this brief in v72; a sheet the owner approves can still
change it.

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 5 — BOILER
MODULES". Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: below the cistern, an old boiler room, riveted steel and fire. The
arena is a deck of riveted steel plates, 46 × 28 m, with grates the fire glows
through, seen from above at about 30°. Steam blobs (STEAMA) live here, and the
boss is KETTLE.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and lathes.
- The floor stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing stands along the near side.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Boiler: a riveted steel drum standing on end, 2.6 m across, 3.2 m to the
   shoulder and a domed top, three bands of rivets round it.
2. Firebox door: an arched iron door at the boiler's foot, barred, the fire
   glowing through it, and the warm light it throws on the deck.
3. Stack: a thin chimney, 2.4 m, out of the boiler's dome, a cap on it, steam
   going up.
4. Steam pipe run: two pipes along each long side, 0.85 m and 0.6 m across,
   a flange every 3.2 m.
5. Valve: a red valve wheel on a short riser off the top pipe, letting off a
   puff of steam now and then.
6. Hazard band: a yellow-and-black striped band, 0.55 m wide, round the deck.
7. Tank: a smaller riveted tank, 1.6 m across, 2 m tall, behind the pipes.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the deck from above at 30°, the boilers along
   the far side, the pipes and tanks down both sides, the band round it.
