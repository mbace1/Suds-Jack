# Art sheet request: WORLD 4 — CISTERN MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31: "continue", after World 3). Same format as the
World 3 request and the approved World 2 sheet. Claude built World 4 from this
brief in v71; a sheet the owner approves can still change it.

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 4 — CISTERN
MODULES". Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: below the sewer, an old flooded cistern, a forest of stone columns
standing in still dark water, lit by warm lamps at their feet. The arena is a
stone platform, 52 × 28 m, a thin sheet of water over it, seen from above at
about 30°. Bubbling blobs (BUBBLA) live here, and the boss is BLISTER.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and lathes.
- The platform stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing stands along the near side.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Stone column: a square base block, a round shaft tapering slightly, and a
   flared capital with a square slab on top; 5.5 m tall, 1.3 m across the shaft.
2. Column foot in water: the wet dark band at the waterline and the ripple
   rings round it.
3. Lamp: a small warm lamp on a short iron bracket at a column's foot, and the
   pool of warm light it throws on the water.
4. Platform edge: a low stone kerb round the platform, 0.2 m tall, 0.5 m wide.
5. Flooded floor: square stone slabs, 2.4 m, under a thin sheet of water with
   caustic light moving over it.
6. Column stump: the cut top of a fallen column set into the floor, a round
   stone disc in a square plinth.
7. Drip rings: rings spreading on the floor's water where a drip falls from the
   vault above.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the platform from above at 30°, two rows of
   columns along the far side and both long sides, lamps at the inner row, the
   dark water all round.
