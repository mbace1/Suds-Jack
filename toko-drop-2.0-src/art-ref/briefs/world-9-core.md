# Art sheet request: WORLD 9 — CORE MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31: "you can build all levels/worlds now"). Same
format as the World 3 and 4 requests and the approved World 2 sheet. Claude
built World 9 from this brief in v72; a sheet the owner approves can still
change it.

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 9 — CORE
MODULES". Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: the machine at the bottom of everything, the last world. The arena
is a black floor traced with circuitry, 38 × 22 m, rings of light going out
from its middle, seen from above at about 30°. Mirror blobs (MIRRA) live here,
and the boss is OMEGA. Every world's rule comes back in turn.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and lathes.
- The floor stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing stands along the near side.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Tower: a dark block, 0.6–1.4 m across and 1.4–5.2 m tall, windows lit in
   bands, a cap on it.
2. Antenna: a thin mast, 0.8–1.8 m, on a tower's cap, a light blinking at its
   tip.
3. Neon ring: a thin hoop of light round the tallest towers, a brighter light
   chasing round it.
4. Light pillar: a beam of light 4.5 m tall standing on a glowing pad.
5. Traced deck: a dark deck with a grid of traces, power running in along them
   toward the floor.
6. Edge: the floor's glowing edge, light running round it.
7. Circuit floor: traces and nodes, lit as the rings pass.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the floor from above at 30°, towers along the
   far side and both sides, the pillars along the far side.
