# Toko Drop 2.0 — the itch upload, ready

- `toko-drop-2-v141-itch.zip`: the HTML5 build. It boots in an itch-style frame, plays ARCADE and a campaign room, and copies a PLAY RECORD with a tap (`scripts/itch-check.mjs`).
- `screenshots/`: the nine arcade worlds, the campaign grid and a boss room, 1920 × 1080, from the v141 zip itself (re-shot 2026-11-10: the driveway to its concept, the blockers, the broken ice, the floors of worlds 3 to 9).
- `clips/`: GIFs of the five clips (`scripts/itch-clips.mjs`), re-cut at v141 (2026-11-10: the floors, blockers and broken ice of v137 to v141 are in them), without the camera's shake: the nine worlds, CURB KING, HARDCADE, a campaign room, and the 28-second progress trailer. The MP4s are rendered by the same script; itch takes a trailer as a YouTube or Vimeo link.
- The cover is `../itch-cover/cover-core@2x.png` (the owner's pick).
- The page text and the upload settings are `docs/ITCH.md` in the source bundle. Price and release status are the owner's.
