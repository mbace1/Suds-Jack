TURF

Nordic street tactics — three operators, one backlot, no way out but through.

Tap an operator, then tap a tile to move or an enemy to hit — move and act in either order, once each, per operator. Full cover (dumpsters, fences) blocks a shot outright; low cover (crates, curbs) only softens one. Every enemy shows what it plans to do before it does it — read the board, not the RNG. Clear the block to win; lose your whole crew and it's over.
