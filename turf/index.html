TURF

Nordic street tactics — three operators, no way out but through.

Tap an operator, then tap a tile to move or an enemy to hit — move and act in either order, once each, per operator. Full cover (dumpsters, fences) blocks a shot outright; low cover (crates, curbs) only softens one. Moving builds MOMENTUM — one pip per tile — which either sharpens your next swing or keeps you hard to shoot until you use it, so standing still is a choice you pay for. Every enemy shows what it plans to do before it does it — read the board, not the RNG. Every shot shows its odds and its damage before you commit. The objective changes: most blocks are HELD, but some ask you to get the crew OUT across green tiles, or to break a cache behind the line — and those run on a clock. Lose the crew and it's over.

Moving builds MOMENTUM — one pip a tile — and momentum buys ACTIONS: cleave a crowd, shove a body into a stairwell, drop cover, or hold your fire on overwatch and shoot the first rival who crosses your line. Spend it on an action or keep it and be harder to shoot; never both.

Mouse or touch · drag to look around · arrows/WASD move, Enter/Space confirm, Esc cancel, E ends turn · pad: stick move, A confirm, B cancel, Y ends turn · AUTO lets the crew play itself
