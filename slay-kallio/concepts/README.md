## Selection rule for Slay Kallio

Prioritize concepts that become *better* when flattened into a horizontal game: strong readable profile, clear forward/back leg separation, weapon silhouettes that extend along the play axis, clothing/hair with useful trailing motion, and distinctive Helsinki/Kallio street identity.

Do not inherit TURF's isometric camera rules. Slay Kallio should establish its own side-view master pose and animation grammar first.
