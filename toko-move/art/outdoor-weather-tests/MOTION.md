# Motion boundaries — conservative, not a rig

Coordinates and bounds below are at 1024 × 576. Full-frame transparent PNGs share an origin; do not trim on import.

| Element | Safe treatment | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated illustration | Hold, dissolve, whole-composite camera scale | **0 px independent body, architecture or tram translation** |
| Courier identity | Locked to underlying head | **0 px independent translation, 0° rotation**; ring is a composited replacement, not a neck/head reconstruction |
| Rain cool grade | Animate opacity between 0 and 100% of supplied overlay | No independent spatial movement |
| Pavement sheen | Fade opacity, keep fixed | **0 px translation**; broad exclusion regions protect figures, not exact rotoscoping |
| Rain streaks | Fade or short diagonal movement | ±2 px X / ±6 px Y with wrap/tile or 2% overscan; no unfilled frame edges |
| Whole finished composite | Gentle push, all layers parented together | Scale 100–102%, keep center; no pan revealing beyond canvas |

Weather toggling/crossfading is the intended first motion test. Rain has a courier-face exclusion so the brand stays legible; moving streaks too far would break that exclusion. Re-seed or regenerate a proper looping weather pass for sustained animation, rather than claiming this single rain frame is a seamless loop.

No body/architecture mattes are delivered. Independent courier walk cycles, cloth motion, parcel hand movement, vehicle glides and multi-plane parallax require redraws/separate clean plates first. There are no reconstructed pixels behind the humans and no hidden limb art. Weather layers can be transferred now; character/environment separation remains a subsequent production task, not an approved capability of these assets.

Visual inspection: same geometry and hand placement across each dry/rain pair; identity is composited from the traced loops rather than generation. Rain keeps the parcel readable. Source plates have subtle raster texture and simplified ordinary faces. Broad sheen exclusions can leave relatively dry areas around figures; keep sheen subtle. No claim of exact Helsinki signage, road layout or transit access geometry.
