# Toko Move v2 owner direction — 2026-08-29

Toko Move is now a Central Helsinki delivery challenge.

Primary objective: complete authored A→B delivery jobs across a recognizable central Helsinki board. The existing city transport network is infrastructure the player plans around; player-drawn lines are interventions that fill gaps and solve delivery routes.

For v2, delivery completion is the primary win condition. Access, emissions and general commuter throughput remain useful simulation signals, but they are secondary telemetry rather than the release's scored objective.

Scope: central Helsinki only. The board should prioritize recognizable geography and transport relationships over street-level cartographic precision.
