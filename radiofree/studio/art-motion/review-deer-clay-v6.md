# Review: "Unexpectedly, a Deer" v6 (clay), 37.5 s

**Verdict: FIX. The fixes are small: two are needed and the rest are optional.** This is clearer and funnier than v5. The price tag pays off and the antlered reflection is the best image in the film. One of the new jokes (the antler check) doesn't read. With no end slate, nothing in the film tells the viewer that the phone line is real.

**The message:** a $20-a-month AI camera writes up your day and confidently tells you your dog is a deer. It lands, and it is now carried by the price tag and the DOG/DEER payoff.

Checked against the stills, the 1 fps contact sheet and frames at 21, 22.5, 24, 33 and 37.3 s. The film is silent, so there is no sound review.

## Fixes

1. **High: caption 5 has to say the line is real.** There is no end slate now, so the film never says that the phone's sentence is a reviewer's real output. Viewers will take it as our gag. "Not a one-off" after a fictional Toko also makes it sound like two incidents.
   *Fix:* `Real line. A reviewer's` / `dogs were logged as [deer],` / `clip after clip.` / `Other owners' cats: raccoons.` (each line ≤ 29 characters). Put the sources (Ars Technica, Android Authority, PCWorld, and "Doodle Home = Google's Gemini for Home") in the post text, since the slate that used to carry them is gone.
2. **High, at 19.3–25.7 s: the antler check doesn't read as a hand.** The magenta mitt sits on the dog's crown as a red ball with a dark cuff. No arm connects it to Toko, whose other hand is on the phone in his lap, so it reads as a pompom or a clown nose. It also covers "$20" on the price tag.
   *Fix:* bring the sleeve round in front of the dog's head from Toko's shoulder. Pat once on each side of the ears, where antlers would be. Roll the dog's eyes up toward the hand. Move the hand or the tag so the tag stays readable.
3. **Medium: caption 3, "One line, word for word:".** The card shows one sentence, so "One line" reads as if that sentence were the whole brief. A real brief is a paragraph.
   *Fix:* `A line from it, verbatim:`
4. **Medium, at 25.7–32.1 s: the RACCOON flag labels the reflection.** It is pinned over the mirror glass on top of the reflected cat, not on the cat itself. The shot table says it "drops onto it". The ringed tabby tail is a good, fair touch: it shows why the mistake happens.
   *Fix:* drop the flag onto the real cat's back, the way the DEER flag sits in the dog in shot 6.
5. **Low, at 6.4–12.9 s: the camera swivels to follow the dog.** Nest Cams don't pan, so this goes beyond the documented behaviour.
   *Fix:* fix the camera in place and let the LED and the brackets do the tracking.
6. **Low, at about 32–34 s: Toko's mask disappears while he bows.** As he leans over to tie the tag, it shows as a red band with two white drawstrings, which looks like a walrus.
   *Fix:* keep the mask at three-quarter view, or lower the camera.
7. **Low, shots 2 and 6: the bottom bracket corners are stubs or dots.** In shot 6 they look like stray red pixels.
   *Fix:* draw full L-corners.

- **DEER flags:** the stack of three carries no count.
