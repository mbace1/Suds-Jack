# Review v4: "On Hold" (Doodle #2) and "A Handful of People" (Coxon #1)

Independent editorial review of the caption rewrites, 14.10.2026. Doodle = Google. Facts were checked with web search; direct page fetches are blocked here, so search excerpts are the evidence (sources at the end).

## Film A, "On Hold": **FIX**

The argument is now clear. One chronology error must be fixed: the felling is tagged and placed **after** 9 September, but it happened **from March to August**.

### 1. Facts

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2025 hold on two Finnish sites until the tax was known | **OK.** Alphabet told the government its Kajaani and Muhos plans were on hold until the tax details were out (HS via Yle 74-20189966; DCD). The Yle article ID dates to about October 2025, so the tag 10.2025 is consistent. |
| 2 | Tax raised anyway, about ×45, from 1.7.2026 | **OK.** Data centres moved from class II (0.05 c/kWh) to class I (2.24 c/kWh) from 1.7.2026 (Valtioneuvosto; VaVM 28/2025). 2.24 / 0.05 = 44.8. The press says "40-fold" because it counts the rise (2.19) rather than the ratio. Defensible. |
| 3 | Government dropped the support (up to €30 M a year), 1.9.2026 | **OK.** Purra announced it after the 2027 budget talks (Xinhua/The Star 2.9.2026, figures via Yle). |
| 4 | Eight days later, €13 bn in Finland | **OK.** 9.9.2026, "at least €13 bn" over 2027–28 in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala (Evertiq, Pulse2, AFP/Macau Business). "In Finland", not "for Finland", is right. |
| 5 | "Felling went ahead at Kajaani and Muhos", tag **9.9.2026** | **ERROR (dating).** HS (via Kaleva) and MTV report the Muhos felling **began in March 2026 and ran until August**. In April Tuike asked to be exempted from the assessment; in May it was told one was needed. Placed after the cheque and tagged 9.9.2026, the shot says the felling followed the announcement. It did not. Kajaani's dates are not reported. The areas are about 330 ha at Muhos and just under 200 ha at Kajaani, "felled or prepared" (LVV via AFP). |
| 6 | "What stopped it… environmental law: the regulator, 6 October" | **Mostly OK.** LVV (6.10) told Tuike Finland to suspend preparatory works immediately, and by 23.10 at the latest, until the environmental impact assessment is done (YVA Act §32(2)). Google said it "fallen short of our own high standards" and would "follow their guidance" (Euronews/AFP). Two problems: (a) "it" grammatically means the *felling*, which at Muhos had already ended in August; (b) compliance is not yet confirmed, since Tuike's reply is due today, 14.10. "For now" carries (b). Fix (a) by wording. |

The station's framing has independent support: DataCenter Knowledge headlined the story "Permits, Not Power, Halt Google's Finland Data Centers".

### 2. Fairness

Doodle is a company, not a person, and the implied "the hold was leverage" reading rests on the public record (hold → tax → no support → €13 bn anyway). The film does not mention that Google disputes illegality: it says it acted under the Forestry Act. The film also does not say Doodle broke the law. If caption 6 gives the reason (felling before the assessment), keep it a statement of sequence, not "illegal". A police preliminary investigation at Muhos exists (Iltalehti), but leave it off screen.

### 3. Clarity

A viewer with no background takes away: "Google threatened to hold off over a tax, got the tax with no support, invested anyway, and only environmental law has halted it."

That is clear. The one gap is that caption 6 does not say **why** environmental law applied. "The regulator, 6 October" is a citation, not a reason.

### 4. Caption fixes (all lines ≤ 26 characters, measured)

- **3** (line 1 is 27 characters):
  - Then the government · [dropped] the support
  - meant to soften the blow.
- **5** (fixes the dating; tag **9.9.2026 → 2026 · KAJAANI · MUHOS**):
  - The higher tax · didn't stop Doodle.
  - Its site work went on at · [Kajaani and Muhos].
  - Optional: have the gate already open rather than swinging open after the calendar.
- **6** (gives the reason; avoids "it" meaning the finished felling):
  - What halted it, for now, · was [environmental law]:
  - the forest was felled · before the assessment.
  - The tag LVV · 6.10.2026 keeps the date.
- **Before publishing:** check whether Tuike's 14.10 reply confirms the halt. If it refuses, change "halted" to "ordered a halt to".

## Film B, "A Handful of People": **SHIP after one line-length fix**

Every figure checks out. The US framing is now unmistakable, and Coxon is treated fairly.

### 1. Facts

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coxon quit Anthropic in September and wrote why | **OK.** He resigned on 8 or 9 Sept 2026 (outlets differ). The X post says "Neither company is acting responsibly" (Fortune 9.9.2026, Common Dreams, Outlook Business). Anthropic is a US lab. |
| 2 | On The Daily Show he told Stewart the choice shouldn't rest with a handful of people | **Appearance OK** (Monday 5.10.2026; IBTimes, AI Weekly). **The "handful of people" wording I could not re-find**: the search surfaced neither the Substack transcription nor the Parade piece the shot table cites. It is consistent with his verified post, which says the gamble should not be launched "from a private company's Slack". **Before publishing, check the paraphrase against the broadcast clip.** It is correctly not in quote marks, and Stewart is not said to agree. |
| 3 | 62% of US voters say AI could get out of control and risk humankind's future | **OK.** Reuters/Ipsos, registered voters, published 7.10.2026 (The Star/Reuters). |
| 4 | 54% say Congress isn't taking AI risks seriously enough | **OK.** The poll wording is "not seriously enough or at all" (same poll; WJLA/Sinclair). |
| 5 | The US House Speaker says Congress won't take the lead | **OK.** This is the Axios headline of 13.9.2026. On CNN Johnson said Congress intervening "isn't the answer" and that the companies can halt their work. He was still refusing to recall the House on 28.9 (Axios Hill Leaders). |
| 6 | Campaign issue; midterms 3 November | **OK.** Reuters 7.10; 3.11.2026 is the Tuesday. Counterweight, not needed on screen: NYT/Siena finds under 1% name AI as their top issue. |

### 2. Fairness

- **Coxon.** On Meet the Press he said Congress should let the frontier labs regulate themselves *while* lawmakers build a long-term framework (NBC, 13–14.9).
  - Johnson's line is partly close to that interim stance, since he says the companies can halt their work themselves. Their real difference is that Coxon wants Congress building the framework now and Johnson says Congress won't lead. Caption 5 is aimed at exactly that difference, so it is fair.
  - Residual risk: a viewer could read "a handful of people" as "the labs must not self-regulate". Nothing on screen says that, and the line is his own point. Acceptable.
- **Stewart.** He is not given a position.
- **Johnson.** Accurate paraphrase of the record. The film omits that he "called for AI solutions" and proposed meeting the CEOs; fine for satire.

### 3. Clarity

A viewer takes away: "An insider quit and says AI's big decisions shouldn't rest with a few people. Most US voters are worried and think Congress isn't serious, the Speaker says Congress won't lead, so it is on voters at the 3 November midterms." Clear.

The only soft word is **"the choice"**: which choice? The post card above it supplies the context, so this is acceptable.

### 4. Caption fixes

- **1** (line 2 is 28 characters):
  - In September, Jacob Coxon · quit US AI lab Anthropic
  - and wrote [why].
- **2** (optional, only if the clip supports it): "Jon Stewart the choice" → "Jon Stewart AI's future" (23 characters), to answer "which choice". Do not change it unless the broadcast backs the broader reading.
- Every other line measures 14–26 characters and fits.
