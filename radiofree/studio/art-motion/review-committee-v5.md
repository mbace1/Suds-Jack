# Review: "Committee of the Whole" v5 captions

**Verdict: SHIP.** All six captions check out against the sources. One fix is optional (caption 6). Nothing in the film is unfair to Coxon or to the companies.

## Per caption

1. **OK.** "On 5 October, the [New York City Council] / met as a committee of all 51 members, on AI risk."
   - The caption still describes **membership**, not attendance: "a committee of all 51 members" says what the committee is made of. The banner's ALL 51 MEMBERS reads the same way.
   - 5 Oct 2026 was a Monday.
   - Sources: council press release of 28 Sept (via search); unite.ai; Interesting Engineering.
   - Naming the council, together with the CITY HALL · NEW YORK tag, answers the owner's question about where this is.

2. **OK.** "It invited five AI companies. Meta said yes. / Three came only under the [threat of subpoenas]."
   - (a) There were exactly **five** letters, sent 15–17 Sept, to the CEOs of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, **SpaceXAI** and Meta. SpaceXAI was one of the original five. It never answered the letter and was subpoenaed on 28 Sept.
   - (b) "Meta said yes" is accurate. Meta agreed without any subpoena threat and said a senior leader would attend.
   - "Threat" is the right word for the other three. Google and Anthropic declined by the 25 Sept deadline, and OpenAI had not confirmed. All three agreed on 27 Sept, Anthropic hours before its subpoena was due.
   - The arithmetic carries the viewer through the film: 1 + 3 = the "four companies" in caption 4, and the fifth is caption 6.
   - Sources: unite.ai; Interesting Engineering; City & State / Route Fifty; council press release of 28 Sept.

3. **OK.** Coxon's caption is unchanged and keeps the qualifier "On our current path, I think" inside the quote. Source: AP / NBC NY.

4. **OK.** Unchanged. Sources: AP, NY1, City & State.

5. **OK.** "Council Speaker Julie Menin called OpenAI's answer "[flippant at best].""
   - (c) Menin is the Speaker. The council's own release is headlined "Speaker Menin issues rare subpoena…".
   - The quote is the verbatim fragment. It was said to OpenAI's witness, Morgan Dwyer, after she said she did not know the odds and the exact number did not matter.
   - Sources: council press release; City & State; AP; ToolNavs summary.

6. **OK, with an optional fix.** "SpaceXAI did not come, despite a [subpoena]. / The council is pursuing it in court."
   - (d) Supported. La Voce di New York (5 Oct) reports that SpaceXAI skipped the hearing and that Menin said the council is pursuing "legal action", which she confirmed means court. The hearing transcript quoted in the shot table says "we are pursuing that subpoena in court". The 28 Sept release named the New York State Supreme Court as the enforcement route.
   - **Optional fix:** now that the sentence's subject is SpaceXAI, "it" reads as the company rather than the subpoena. Either reading is true, since enforcing a subpoena means taking the company to court. To mirror the transcript exactly: `The council is pursuing` / `the subpoena in court.` (23 and 21 characters).

## Fairness

- No problems.
- One search excerpt, unconfirmed, says SpaceXAI answered the *subpoena* with a letter offering cooperation but not attendance. Nothing on screen contradicts that:
  - the blank envelope stands for the unanswered *invitation*, which the council's release confirms;
  - "did not come" is true either way.
  - Do not add "never replied" anywhere.
- The skyline is a composite. From the front of City Hall, Woolworth stands to the side and One WTC is behind the viewer. That is acceptable stagecraft for papercut, and it is not presented as a photograph.

## Line length

- "It invited five AI companies." is 29 characters, one over the guide. Lines of 31 characters have already rendered at about 720 px, so it is acceptable as is.
- Every other new line is 28 characters or fewer.

## What a viewer with no background takes away

New York's entire city council held a hearing on AI risk. Of five AI firms invited, only Meta came willingly, three came under threat of subpoena, and one skipped despite a subpoena. An ex-Anthropic insider warned that humanity may lose control. None of the firms would put a number on the risk, and the Speaker called OpenAI's dodge "flippant at best".
