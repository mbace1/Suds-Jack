# Review: "The Biodiversity Fund" (成片_bdf.mp4)

**Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES, 7/10.** A clear, handsome five-beat argument with frame-accurate cuts. One word must change before it airs, and the punchline needs more screen time.

Measured: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15.000 s. Loudness −14.0 LUFS, true peak −2.3 dBTP, LRA 4.7. Cuts at 3.233 / 6.467 / 9.667 / 12.900 s, all within one frame of plan. Motion per shot (mean frame-diff): 1.87 / 0.85 / 0.47 / 1.90 / 2.52.

## RED

1. **14.0–15.0, "PLANTED 130 HA" states a pledge as done.** The company *said it would* plant 130 ha at Muhos. **Fix:** "PLEDGED 130 HA". The lone seedling then becomes the joke on the pledge.

## YELLOW

1. **13.9–15.0, the punchline is nearly subliminal.** CUT lands at 14.0, PLANTED at 14.1, IMPACT is fully opaque at about 14.35. That leaves under a second for eight words. IMPACT is magenta on brown sky at 1.6–2.0:1 contrast. **Fix:** move the last cut a beat earlier (12.86 → 12.32; shot 4 is static from 11.3) and land the tallies by about 13.3. Put IMPACT on a cream card like the other two.
2. **14.2, "IMPACT ASSESSMENT PENDING" asserts a status nobody has reported.** The reported fact is the 200 ha threshold. **Fix:** "EIA REQUIRED ABOVE 200 HA". It can be checked, and 330 against 200 makes the joke.
3. **0–3.2, the cheque memo.** "PAY Finland … FOR: local biodiversity, education, research…" reads as if all €13 bn went to those causes. In fact the investment *includes* such funds; the ellipsis keeps this from being a RED. The memo is also about 5 pt on a phone, and shot 3's payoff depends on it. **Fix:** "INCL.:" instead of "FOR:", at ≥28 px.
4. **3.4–6.43, the counter.** The "MUHOS · FELLED" label (~16 px) is overlapped by the three-digit numbers. 330 holds for only 0.23 s. **Fix:** put the label above the number at ≥28 px, and land 330 by about 5.9.
5. **The moose.** In shot 1 it is dark on dark, and the head-lift at 1.1–1.4 merges into the body, so "looks up" barely reads. In shot 3 it is half off-frame and reads as a blob. **Fix:** an ochre rim on the back and antlers, with the raised head in profile against the lighter sky band.
6. **12.9–15.0, the data hall reads as a generic factory.** Nothing on screen says data centre or Doodle, so the *why* of the felling is missing. **Fix:** server-rack window stripes and a small DOODLE sign.
7. **9.7–12.86, the desk.**
   - The open ring at bottom right looks like a buffering spinner. Close it into a coffee ring or remove it.
   - The paper clip covers "JA" in the agency name.
   - The header and TO/RE lines only read on pause.
   - The push-in is about 4% and isn't felt. Commit to about 12% or drop it.
8. **Minor.**
   - At 12.87 the mask is half-cropped at the left edge, and leaves cross it at 12.87 and 14.97.
   - No hand is shown setting the seedling.
   - The FUND tag sits flush with the right frame edge, close to a platform's icon rail.

## GREEN

- **Sync.** Hits land on picture: 1.00, 6.95, 7.50, 10.70, 13.90 / 14.05 / 14.20.
- **Ending.** The final hit decays from −6.5 to −65 dB by 14.95 and ends in digital silence. Nothing is chopped. The 0.7 s hush (12.95–13.65) works as a breath.
- **Level.** −14 LUFS is right for social. Drop 2 dB if the clip sits among the −16 LUFS station films.
- **Facts.** All correct: 9.9.2026, €13 bn, 7.10.2026, LUPA- JA VALVONTAVIRASTO, MUHOS · KAJAANI, 23.10, 14.10, 330 ha. Doodle is consistent.
- **Legibility.** Every large word reads at phone size.

## What works

- **Palette restraint.** Ochre is the light and magenta is saved for FUND, the stamp, the mask and the last line, so each of those frames hits.
- **The arc reads without the brief.** Money, felling, greenwash label, regulator, tally. Each shot reads within about a second.
- **The log pile tagged BIODIVERSITY FUND** is the funniest frame and lands on the 7.5 s hit.
- **The stamp** is the best-built beat.
- **The pull-back** from one seedling to a grid of stumps is a strong visual argument that deserves another half-second.
