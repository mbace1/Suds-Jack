## Sources

- The hearing: NYC Council Committee of the Whole, Mon 5 Oct 2026, all 51 members, "Examining the Risks Posed by Artificial Intelligence", with a package of city bills; the council's first Committee of the Whole since 2022. Council press, council.nyc.gov/press/2026/10/05/3278/ and 2026/09/25/3252/; The Next Web, "At NYC's AI hearing, ex-lab insiders warn humanity may lose control of AI"; 6sqft.
- Attendance: letters went to five companies 15–17 Sept; Meta confirmed; at the 25 Sept deadline Google and Anthropic declined; after Speaker Menin authorised subpoenas, OpenAI and Google agreed, and Anthropic confirmed late on the Sunday, hours before service; the council says the companies confirmed only after its explicit threat of subpoena, Meta excepted. unite.ai, "NYC Council hearing puts Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta under oath"; Interesting Engineering; City & State.
- SpaceXAI: subpoenaed 28 Sept 2026 (council press 2026/09/28/3266); at the hearing the council said the company was "not here at all in direct violation of the subpoena that we issued last week, and we are pursuing that subpoena in court" (hearing transcript, rev.com/transcripts/nyc-ai-hearing). An agreement to appear later was reported in one research note but NOT confirmed by any search excerpt — not used.
- Coxon: "On our current path, I think it is more likely than not that humanity loses control to these AIs, and it could end in human extinction." NBC New York; Click2Houston (AP), 5.10.2026; AFP via Korea Times ("on the current path"). The caption quotes the fragment that every version shares. He left Anthropic in September 2026 after OpenAI (2023–26): his resignation post, 8/9 Sept 2026; Axios 9.9.2026.
- "Flippant at best" was said to OpenAI's answer (its witness did not know the odds and said the exact number did not matter): City & State; AP via Click2Houston; NY1.
- The odds: Speaker Menin asked each company to put a number on the worst-case catastrophic risk; none of the four gave one (Google's witness: forecasting it "is not a perfect science at this stage"; OpenAI's said no level is acceptable and the percentage is not the point). Menin called the answer "flippant at best" — the fragment every outlet carries; one account has "To say you don't know and it doesn't matter is flippant at best". "I don't know" as OpenAI's own words could NOT be confirmed — not quoted. NBC New York; Click2Houston; NY1; PYMNTS; The Next Web.
