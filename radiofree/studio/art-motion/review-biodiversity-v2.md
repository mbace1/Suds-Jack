# Review: "The Biodiversity Fund" v2 (成片_bdf_v2.mp4)

**Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES, 7/10.** The new opening gives the film a real argument and the old RED is gone, but two new text problems must be fixed first.

Measured: 18.2 s, −14.1 LUFS, true peak −0.9 dBTP. All five cuts are within a frame of the 3.214 s grid.

## RED

1. **16.1–18.2 s: two events fused into one image.** "50 000 SIGNATURES · IN DAYS" sits above a masked crowd at Doodle's Arkadiankatu office while paper rains onto the door. It reads as 50 000 people at Doodle, or a petition to Doodle. In fact about 100 people protested there on 12.10 (organised by Luontoliitto). *Datakeskukset kuriin* is a separate citizens' initiative **to parliament** on data-centre grid limits and a fee. **Fix:** "50 000 SIGNED · CITIZENS' INITIATIVE TO PARLIAMENT", and take the falling sheets off the office. Optionally tag the crowd "≈100 · 12.10".
2. **12.9–16.1 s: an unsourced quote on the agency's letter.** "Until the environmental impact assessment is complete." is not in the shot list's sources, which only support suspending the work by 23.10. On LVV's letterhead it reads as a quote, and a grey rule runs through it, so it also looks struck out. **Fix:** source it or cut it. The stamp carries the beat.

## YELLOW

1. **The Helsinki crowd wears the Muhos residents' costumes.** Same green coats, rust scarves and mask straps, so the protest reads as "the locals rose up". The shot list says the film must not claim that. **Fix:** give the crowd different coats. The residents' strap also reads as a blindfold.
2. **"ARKADIANKATU · HELSINKI" is about 16 px (≈6 pt).** A phone viewer won't know it's Helsinki. Set it at ≥30 px. "Data centre" no longer appears anywhere either; the RED-1 label can carry it.
3. **0.3–1.0 s: all seven hands go up.** The sources don't say the vote was unanimous. If it wasn't, leave one hand down.
4. **True peak −0.9 dBTP** (v1 was −2.3). Aim for about −1.5 to survive platform re-encodes.
5. **The moose.** The back-glow helps, but there is still no edge rim. A hard-edged light patch between its legs (4.3–6.4 s) reads as a box. In shot 4 it is still half off frame.

## GREEN

- **Facts.** These check out: KUNNANVALTUUSTO, APPROVED, 1 APPEAL, the INCL. memo, 330 ha, 7.10 / 23.10 / 14.10, Arkadiankatu, "in days".
- **No overclaim about locals.** Nothing says they oppose it; the residents watch rather than protest. The crowd carries no slogans, and about 25 visible figures fits ~100 present.
- **Sync.** Hits land on picture: APPROVED 1.05 s, APPEAL 2.10, cheque 4.20, stamp 13.90, placards 17.35.
- **Ending.** The last hit decays to −58 dB by 18.19 s with no audible chop.
- **Legibility.** APPROVED, 1 APPEAL, the counter, SUSPEND and 50 000 all read at phone size.
