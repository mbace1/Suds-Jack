# geo.json

Coastlines, lakes and land borders from **Natural Earth** 10m (land, lakes, admin-0 boundary lines land),
public domain ("Made with Natural Earth", naturalearthdata.com). Fetched 9 Nov 2026 from
github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, clipped to lon -14..64, lat 44..78 and simplified to 0.025 deg; `globe` is every
land outer ring simplified to 0.3 deg. Built by a throwaway script (clip, simplify); no other data.
