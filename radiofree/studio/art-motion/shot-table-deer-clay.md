# Unexpectedly, a Deer — Toko1, v6 (CLAY remake), 37.5 s, portrait 1080×1920, 112 BPM grid

Toko thinker #1, remade in the house clay style (owner, 2026-10-14: "The deer video is funny, needs some clarity of messaging, or more jokes, but works. It should be done in the new clay style"). The papercut v5 stays in the record; this replaces it as the release.

**The message, in one line:** Toko pays $20 a month for a home camera whose AI writes up his day — and it confidently tells him his dog is a deer. The behaviour is real and documented (reviewers' dogs logged as deer, other users' cats as raccoons); the flat, the dog, the cat and Toko are ours.

**What v6 adds for clarity and jokes:** the price is set up in shot 1 (a paper price tag hangs off the camera), so every later DEER is $20-a-month's worth; Toko checks the dog for antlers (shot 4); the dog meets its own reflection — with antlers — and a cat walks in and gets a RACCOON tag (shot 5); the payoff: Toko writes his own label by hand, DOG, and ties it on the collar next to the camera's DEER (shot 6).

画风：House clay (Aardman plasticine, as Happy Little Bot): matte clay with thumbprints and tool drags, chunky forms, a clay-and-card set, warm lamp key against a cold rainy window, shallow depth of field, graded a stop darker; posed on twos (12 poses/s in a 24 fps file) while camera, rain and light move every frame. Set it apart from Happy Little Bot's window seat: this is a Kallio living room — sofa, rug, a bookshelf with the camera, a hallway mirror — with a green-and-yellow Helsinki tram passing in the rain outside.
