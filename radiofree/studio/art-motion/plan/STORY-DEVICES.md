# Story first: find the idea, then build around it (owner, 6 Nov)

**Owner, 6 Nov:** "In general you should be looking at all options to innovate on the storytelling. Seeing an idea and
then creating around that."

**The rule.** A pitch starts with the **idea**: one device that makes this story land. The medium (papercut, clay, 3D
road, room or machine, GL) and the kit are chosen after, to serve the device. A pitch that is "the usual look with new
captions" is not finished. Every pitch to the owner names its device in one line, and where there is a real choice it
offers two or three devices.

## How a pitch is made now

1. Read the record (the check's facts, sources and the company's side).
2. Write **three devices** for it, one line each, from the bank or new ones. New ones go into the bank.
3. Pick the device that is truest to the record *and* the most surprising. Only then pick the medium.
4. Show the owner the device in one line with a still before any full build.
