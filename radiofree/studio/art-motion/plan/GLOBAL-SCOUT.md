  AI-powered weaponry.

## Whistleblowers (owner, 23 Oct: "take whistleblowers as realistic insights into these kinds of cases")

A whistleblower account counts as a source class: congressional or parliamentary testimony, SEC or other regulator
complaints, court filings, open letters by employees, and on-record interviews.

**Rules.**
- **Attribute every claim to the whistleblower, as an allegation.** On screen: *a former engineer says…* or
  *in testimony, …*. Never present it as fact, and paraphrase without quote marks.
- **Note any response:** whether the company has denied or responded, and whether any authority has acted.
- **Use only people who went public themselves.** Never identify an anonymous source.
- **Mark the candidate** with ★WB when a whistleblower is its core insight.

None of the candidates above (#1–#9) rests on a whistleblower. #2's critic (AI Now) is an outside expert, not a
whistleblower. The two below are new, both ★WB.
