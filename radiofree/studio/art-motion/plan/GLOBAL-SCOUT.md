## 1. The Panel · Brink (or Coxon) · peg 6 Oct, the 120-day study runs into Feb 2027 · HIGH

**Pitch.** The Pentagon's study on what weapons to buy next is led by people who sell weapons to the Pentagon.

**The record.**
- The Defense Secretary announced *Project Meridian: The Future of Warfare*, a 120-day study of automated and
  AI-powered weaponry.
- Its three leaders include the CEO of SpaceX and the co-founder of Anduril. Both hold billions of dollars in current
  defense contracts (NPR/AP).
- A former senior DoD official called it a massive conflict of interest. The study could shape which munitions the US
  buys, for example drones or space assets over tanks.
- The DoD says MITRE does the analysis and will apply conflict-of-interest safeguards.
- The DoD has not said whether the two were hired as special government employees, which decides which ethics rules
  apply to them.

**Hook / punch (cold-viewer captions).**
1. The Pentagon asked: / what should we / [buy next]?
2. It asked three people. / Two of them / [sell] to it.
3. Billions in / current defence / [contracts].
4. Which ethics rules apply? / The Pentagon / [hasn't said].

**Sources.**
- NPR, *Elon Musk and Palmer Luckey's new Pentagon roles raise ethics worries*, 6 Oct 2026:
  https://www.npr.org/2026/10/06/nx-s1-5991899/elon-musk-palmer-luckey-pentagon-drones-ai
- Member-station reprints: https://www.wunc.org/2026-10-06/elon-musk-and-palmer-luckeys-new-pentagon-roles-raise-ethics-worries
- Verified from search summary only.

**Risk.** Named public figures, and no finding of wrongdoing. Say only what the record says: who leads it, that they
hold contracts, that the ethics status is undisclosed. Do not call it a conflict ourselves; the conflict-of-interest
line is the former official's, and it would go in the post, attributed and paraphrased. No faces: the figures are
masked Tokos or empty chairs.

**Watch.** The SGE status disclosure, any ethics review, MITRE's safeguards, and the report itself (~Feb 2027).

## Whistleblowers (owner, 23 Oct: "take whistleblowers as realistic insights into these kinds of cases")

A whistleblower account counts as a source class: congressional or parliamentary testimony, SEC or other regulator
complaints, court filings, open letters by employees, and on-record interviews.

**Rules.**
- **Attribute every claim to the whistleblower, as an allegation.** On screen: *a former engineer says…* or
  *in testimony, …*. Never present it as fact, and paraphrase without quote marks.
- **Note any response:** whether the company has denied or responded, and whether any authority has acted.
- **Use only people who went public themselves.** Never identify an anonymous source.
- **Mark the candidate** with ★WB when a whistleblower is its core insight.

None of the candidates above (#1–#9) rests on a whistleblower. #2's critic (AI Now) is an outside expert, not a
whistleblower. The two below are new, both ★WB.
