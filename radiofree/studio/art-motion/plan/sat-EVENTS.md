# Satire Excepted (`sat`): sound events for the composer

The film runs 37.5 s at 112 BPM (one beat = 0.536 s).
- Cuts fall at 6.43, 12.86, 19.29, 25.71 and 32.14. Each is a 0.5 s dissolve starting on the cut.
- All times are absolute seconds in the picture, taken from the scene code (`scenes/sat_*.js`).

Music, from the shot table: a jaunty accordion-and-harpsichord waltz in 3/4 on the 168 grid.
- One waltz bar (3 × 0.357 s) equals two beats of the picture's 112 grid, so each 6.43 s shot is
  exactly six bars and every cut lands on a downbeat.
- The last line gets a little *ta-da*. Leave about 2 s of near-nothing after "This has been satire."
  (32.84). Then the *ta-da* lands on "Probably." at 34.84, as the balance swings in.
- Let the tail ring out to 37.50 and never chop it.

## Shot 1 · 0.00–6.43 · Christiansborg, Copenhagen
- **0.40–4.18** The palace windows light up one by one, candle-warm: 42 of them, one every 0.09 s.
  Use a soft rising sparkle or celesta run, not 42 separate hits.
- **0.50–6.10** A canal tour boat crosses left to right (gentle water lap, a low putter).
- **The whole shot:** two Dannebrogs flutter on their poles (cloth flap) and a string of paper
  flags sways over the water (paper rustle).
- **0.50** The tag FOLKETINGET · COPENHAGEN appears. It needs no sound.

## Shot 2 · 6.43–12.86 · The copying press
- **6.68–7.38** The press's lens reads the masked person's face, a red light sweeping down it. Use a
  scanner hum or shimmer.
- **The four stamps:** the press stamps on beats 2, 4, 6 and 8. Each is a heavy wooden-and-metal
  KA-CHUNK, landing at **7.50 · 8.57 · 9.64 · 10.71**. The platen drops 0.12 s before each, so a
  quick whoosh-down helps.
- **The copies:** each copy hops out of the press to the row along the bottom (paper slide), landing
  at 8.18 · 9.25 · 10.32 · 11.39. In order they are the face, the face again, the voice and the
  moves. The voice is a sound-wave card, so a tiny vocal blip could sit there.
- **The © tags:** each copy's © tag pops on 0.05 s after it lands (a small clip or ting), at
  **8.23 · 9.30 · 10.37 · 11.44**.
- A candle flickers on the wall shelf and needs no sound.

## Shot 3 · 12.86–19.29 · The bill
- **13.39** The bill (L 49) slides in and slaps down on the desk (paper slide plus slap). It is
  tabled, literally.
- **The calendar:** pages tear off the wall calendar at **14.46 · 14.87 · 15.27** (three paper
  rips: October to November to December to January).
- **14.86–17.46** A slow push-in.
- **16.07–16.62** A red pencil circles the "1" of JANUARY 2027 (a pencil scribble, one loop). Make it
  a light hit, because this is a target date, not a result.
- **0.50 into the shot (13.36)** The tag BILL L 49 · TABLED 8.10.2026 appears. It needs no sound.
- A candle on the desk flickers.

## Shot 4 · 19.29–25.71 · The rail of faces
- **The whole shot:** masks sway gently on their hangers (a faint wooden creak).
- **20.89–21.34** The © tag on the plain card mask flips over: SATIRE ✓ PARODY ✓. Use a card flip
  with a bright two-note *ding-ding*, one per tick. This is the hit of the shot.
- **21.19–23.39** The camera leans in on the tag.

## Shot 5 · 25.71–32.14 · The balance
- **25.87** The laughing card face falls into the left pan.
- **26.25** It lands (a brass *clank*), and the beam tips left.
- **26.41** The hurt face falls into the right pan.
- **26.79** It lands (a clank). The MISLEADS tag swings beneath it.
- **26.79–32.14** The beam never settles. It see-saws in a slow wobble, about one swing every 1.4 s,
  for the rest of the shot. A gentle ticking or a creak on each turn works: a court decides case by
  case.

## Shot 6 · 32.14–37.50 · Toko signs off (the joke)
- **32.34–33.29** Toko takes the plain card mask on its stick off the rail (the hanger swings and
  rattles) and lifts it over his own face (a little flourish). Its tag reads SATIRE ONLY.
- **32.84** "This has been satire." Then almost silence for 2 s; the gap is the joke.
- **34.84 "Probably."** The *ta-da*. At the same moment the brass balance from shot 5 swings in on its
  cord from the right, as a pendulum: a whoosh, then a creaking swing. It settles beside him from
  about 35.5, its beam see-sawing (soft clinks) to the end.
- **37.15–37.50** The captions fade out. Let the *ta-da* and the creak ring out to 37.50.
