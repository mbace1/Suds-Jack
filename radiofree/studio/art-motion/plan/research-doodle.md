# Doodle series: candidate stories (research, 15 Oct 2026)

Scope: Big Tech doing things that its own record makes ironic, Aug to Oct 2026, with a
Finnish, Nordic or EU angle. In the films Google is "Doodle"; other companies keep their names.
Excluded (already made): Google's Finland data centres up to and including the LVV order of
6 Oct 2026. One genuinely NEW development after 6 Oct is noted at the end (F1).

Method: web search only (page fetches were blocked for most Finnish government and STT
sites). Every fact below was checked against at least two outlets unless it is flagged
**single-source**. Quotes are only ones the outlets attribute. Last lines are the
station's own words and are not quotes from anyone.

Recommended order: **3 (Meta scam ads)**, **1 (Doodle's privacy appeal)**, **4 (Siri speaks
French)**, then 2, 5, 6.

---

## 1. "Irreversible": Doodle defends your search history

**What happened.** In July 2026 the Commission used the DMA to spell out how Google must
share anonymised search data (queries, clicks, views) with rival search engines and AI
chatbots, and how it must open Android to rival AI assistants. On 28 Sept 2026 Google took
both decisions to the EU General Court. It said they would force it to share "people's
private search history without sufficient anonymisation", causing "irreversible harm" to
privacy. By 1 Oct it had also asked the court to suspend the orders while the case runs. The
Commission says the measures contain privacy safeguards and let Google refuse risky
recipients.

**Sources (agree):**
- Euronews, 29 Sept 2026: https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/29/google-appeals-eu-data-sharing-order-warns-of-irreversible-harm
- The Next Web: https://thenextweb.com/news/google-challenges-eu-dma-orders-search-data
- Reuters via The Star, 1 Oct 2026 (interim suspension request): https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/01/google-asks-eu-court-to-suspend-order-to-open-up-to-ai-chatbots-search-engine-rivals
- Background, the law's own wording: DMA Recital 61 defines anonymised data as personal
  data "irreversibly altered" — Wolters Kluwer competition blog
  https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/competition-blog/rolling-with-the-punches-the-european-commissions-approach-to-the-sharing-of-search-data-under-article-611-dma/
  and European Law Blog https://www.europeanlawblog.eu/pub/2uxr4anu
- Optional second beat, the same pattern in the record: the Commission's Dec 2025 antitrust
  probe says YouTube uploaders must license their videos to Google, including for AI
  training, while YouTube bars rival AI developers from using the same content — The
  Register https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/09/eu_google_ai_antitrust/ and EU
  Perspectives https://euperspectives.eu/2025/12/eu-investigates-google-over-use-of-publisher-and-youtube-content-in-ai/

**Irony / insight.** The law Google is appealing already requires the data to be
*irreversibly* anonymised before it leaves. The company warning about "irreversible harm" to
your search history is the company that holds it.

**Last line.** "Doodle has gone to court to protect your search history. From everyone except Doodle."

**Image.** A search bar with a padlock snapping shut on its front, while a ticker tape of
typed searches keeps feeding out of the back into one pocket.

**Risks.**
- The case is pending. Do not say Google's privacy argument is false. Present both sides:
  Google says the anonymisation is not enough, and the Commission says safeguards exist.
- The named Google executive is not needed. Attribute the quote to "Google".
- Dates vary slightly between sources: the July decision date (16 July) and when sharing
  starts (Jan 2027 in one source).

## 2. Doodle complies: the free part is gone

**What happened.** On 22/23 July 2026 the Commission fined Google €890m under the DMA:
€460m for putting its own shopping, hotel, transport and sports results first in Search,
and €430m over Play anti-steering. Google had 60 days to comply, until about 21 Sept. Between
15 and 18 Sept, free product listings and "popular products" carousels vanished from Google
results across the EEA (trackers measured 90 to 100% drops in Germany, France, Belgium, the
Netherlands and Sweden), while **paid Shopping ads kept running**. Separately, from
**30 Aug 2026** Google stopped applying its "site reputation abuse" spam penalties to users
in the EEA (the EU plus Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein) to settle a separate DMA probe,
and it keeps them everywhere else. On **14 Oct 2026** the Commission confirmed it is still
in talks with Google and assessing the changes.

**Sources.**
- Free listings removed: Search Engine Roundtable https://www.seroundtable.com/google-drops-free-product-listings-eea-42113.html ;
  Search Engine Watch https://searchenginewatch.com/google-drops-free-listings-and-carousels-from-the-european-economic-area/ ;
  PPC Land https://ppc.land/google-drops-free-shopping-listings-across-europe-in-two-days/
- Spam policy switched off for the EEA: RTÉ (wire), 28 Aug 2026 https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0828/1589545-google-changes-spam-policy/ ;
  Search Engine Land https://searchengineland.com/google-wont-respect-manual-actions-for-site-reputation-abuse-in-european-economic-area-486055
- Fine and the talks on 14 Oct: The Next Web https://thenextweb.com/news/eu-google-dma-fine-talks ;
  Commission press release https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1670

**Irony / insight.** After being told to stop favouring its own results, Google's EEA search
lost the free shopping results and kept the paid ones. A spam penalty presented as protecting
searchers turned out to stop at the EEA border.

**Last line.** "Doodle has complied with European law. Everything free has been removed for your safety."

**Image.** A shop shelf that tilts: every item without a price tag slides off the edge, and
the priced ones stay as if glued.

**Risks.**
- **The free-listings removal is reported only by trade/SEO press** (no wire or broadsheet
  found). Its link to DMA compliance rests on a second-hand account of a Google ads
  spokesperson. The 14 Oct Reuters item confirms only that Google "started altering search
  result displays".
- Google's motive is not on the record. Do not say "to punish Europe". Say what changed and
  what stayed.
- The Commission has not ruled on whether the changes comply.

## 3. The newsreader was fake (Meta, scam ads, Finnish faces)

**What happened.** In April 2026 news anchors from Yle and MTV ran a joint campaign asking
Meta to stop scam ads that used their faces and names, often to sell crypto. In an OP
Pohjola survey (May 2026), Facebook was the place Finns most often saw ads they suspected
were scams, followed by Meta's other apps. On **16 Sept 2026** the Frankfurt Regional Court
held Meta directly liable for investment-scam ads impersonating a German finance brand. The
court rejected Meta's "passive host" defence because Meta's own ad ranking and auction decide
who sees what. The judgment is not final. Background from the record: in Nov 2025 Reuters
reported that Meta's internal documents projected about 10% of 2024 revenue (about $16bn)
from scam and banned-goods ads. The same documents showed that advertisers Meta suspected of
fraud, but was not 95% sure about, were charged **higher** rates ("penalty bids") rather
than removed. Meta called the estimates "rough and overly-inclusive".

**Sources.**
- Yle on the Reuters documents: https://yle.fi/a/74-20193060 ; PPC Land on penalty bids:
  https://ppc.land/meta-charged-suspected-fraudsters-premium-rates-while-earning-billions-from-scam-ads/
- Yle and MTV anchors' campaign: Yle https://yle.fi/a/74-20220249 ; MTV Uutiset
  https://www.mtvuutiset.fi/artikkeli/yle-ja-mtv-yhdistivat-voimansa-katso-video-uutisankkurit-tempaisevat-valheita-vastaan/9323828
- Frankfurt ruling: The Next Web https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-scam-ads-ruling-germany-frankfurt-court ;
  Technology.org https://www.technology.org/2026/09/18/german-court-meta-liable-fake-investment-ads/ ;
  Android Headlines https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/09/meta-held-liable-for-scam-ads-on-instagram-and-facebook-in-court-ruling.html
- OP survey (Finnish): Aamuset https://aamuset.fi/artikkeli/7026684 (**single-source** in this search)

**Irony / insight.** According to Reuters' reading of Meta's own documents, a suspected
fraudster was charged more instead of being turned away. A German court has now said that
Meta's auction is exactly why it is not a bystander.

**Last line.** "The newsreader was fake. The invoice was real."

**Image.** A cardboard cut-out newsreader holding a crypto sign. Each time a passer-by
glances at it, a cash-register drawer behind it springs open.

**Risks.**
- **Do not show or imitate any real Yle/MTV anchor.** Recreating their likeness is the very
  thing the scam does. Use a faceless or obviously cardboard presenter.
- The Reuters figures are Meta's internal estimates, which Meta disputes. Attribute them
  ("Meta's own documents, as reported by Reuters"). "Penalty bids" is Meta's internal term,
  and Meta describes them as a deterrent.
- The Frankfurt judgment can be appealed. The plaintiff brand and its co-founder are named in
  coverage but are not needed.

## 4. Siri speaks French, just not in France (Apple)

**What happened.** On 8 June 2026 Apple said the new "Siri AI" would not come to iPhone,
iPad or Apple Watch in the EU with iOS 27. It blamed the DMA's interoperability rules and
gave no timeline. In the EU it **is** available on macOS 27 and visionOS 27, which the DMA
does not designate. The Commission said the decision was "Apple's and Apple's only" and
that Apple had sought a blanket exemption, which the law does not provide for. iOS 27 shipped
in September without it for EU iPhones. On **16 Sept 2026** the iOS 27.2 beta added Siri AI
in French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean (public beta 3 on 13 Oct), still with no
EU iPhone rollout.

**Sources.**
- Apple Newsroom (June 2026): https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/due-to-dma-siri-ai-delayed-in-eu-for-ios-27-and-ipados-27/
- MacRumors (Commission: Apple's decision): https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/09/eu-says-decision-not-to-launch-siri-ai-in-europe-is-apples/ ;
  The Next Web https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-siri-ai-eu-dma-delay-ios-27
- Why it works on a Mac (DMA scope): Netzwelt https://www.netzwelt.de/news/254894-siri-ai-deutschland-mac-erlaubt-iphone-verboten-grund.html
- New languages: MacRumors https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/16/siri-ai-new-languages-ios-27-2/ ;
  AppleInsider https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/16/new-in-ios-272-beta-redesigned-health-app-siri-ai-localization-more
- The 27.2 beta still excludes EU iPhones: Neoteo https://www.neoteo.com/en/ios-272-beta-adds-five-siri-ai-languages-but-eu-iphones-remain-excluded (**single-source** for 27.2 specifically; Apple's June statement of "no timeline" covers it)

**Irony / insight.** In the EU, the new Siri runs on every Apple device the DMA does not
cover and is missing from the ones it does. This autumn it learned French, Spanish and
Portuguese, and it still cannot use them on an iPhone in France, Spain or Portugal.

**Last line.** "Siri learned French this autumn. In Paris, only the laptop is allowed to say so."

**Image.** A café table: a laptop chattering away in speech bubbles, and beside it a phone
with a strip of tape over its speaker.

**Risks.**
- Apple and the Commission give competing accounts, and Apple frames the issue as user
  privacy and security. Show both sides. The Mac/iPhone split is a fact of DMA scope, not an
  allegation.
- Check the release status before airing, since a rollout could be announced at any time.
- No individuals are needed (coverage names an Apple SVP and a Commission spokesperson).

## 5. The appendix (Microsoft, Finnish state cloud)

**What happened.** On **18 Aug 2026** Finland's Ministry of Finance announced that Valtori,
the state IT centre, has added a more detailed data protection appendix to its Microsoft
agreement. The appendix came out of the Cirrus project, clarifies Microsoft's role as a data
processor, and lets public bodies "continue to use Microsoft services in compliance with EU
and Finnish legislation". Earlier record: in **Oct 2025** the Data Protection Ombudsman found
that personal data sent to the US through Valtori's cloud service in Feb–Apr 2022 had not
been adequately protected, and reprimanded Valtori. In **June 2025** Microsoft France's
legal director told a French Senate inquiry under oath that he could not guarantee French
citizens' data would never be handed to US authorities without French approval (the US
CLOUD Act).

**Sources.**
- Ministry of Finance release, 18 Aug 2026: https://vm.fi/en/-/valtori-clarifies-data-protection-practices-in-its-agreements-with-microsoft
  (also on valtioneuvosto.fi). This is **official single-source**: the appendix text is not public.
- Ombudsman finding (2025): IPRinfo https://iprinfo.fi/uutiset/tsv-valtion-pilvipalveluiden-suoja-ei-ole-ollut-riittavalla-tasolla/ ;
  Valtori's response https://valtori.fi/-/tietosuojavaltuutetun-huomautus-valtorille-tiedonsiirrosta-vuonna-2022-taustalla-juridinen-tulkinta
- Microsoft "cannot guarantee": The Register https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/25/microsoft_admits_it_cannot_guarantee/ ;
  PPC Land https://ppc.land/microsoft-cant-protect-french-data-from-us-government-access/

**Irony / insight.** Finland answered its US-cloud problem with a more detailed contract with
the supplier whose own legal director testified that he cannot guarantee a US order will
never reach the data.

**Last line.** "Finland has a new appendix. America has the same law."

**Image.** A paper label reading LOCKED being neatly stapled onto a padlock whose shackle
is still open.

**Risks.**
- This is a juxtaposition, not an accusation. The ministry's claim concerns GDPR processor
  obligations, and Valtori says its main providers now process data only in the EU/EEA. Do
  not say the ministry is wrong or that data has been handed over. Microsoft said no such
  request had happened (that part is single-sourced).
- The 2025 Ombudsman finding covers 2022, when there was no EU-US transfer framework.
- The Microsoft France official is named in coverage and does not need to be named on air.

## 6. Badge for the fake, price for the copy (Spotify, Stockholm)

**What happened.** On **21 May 2026** Spotify and Universal Music announced licensing deals
for a tool that lets fans make AI covers and remixes of participating artists' songs. It is
planned as a paid Premium add-on with a revenue share, with no price or date announced. On
**11 Aug 2026** Spotify announced "AI Persona" badges, rolling out from mid-September, for
artist profiles whose *identity* is AI-generated. Such profiles are kept out of editorial
and algorithmic recommendations by default. Spotify says the badge is about whether a
profile is a real person, not whether AI made the music.

**Sources.**
- AI Persona badge: Spotify Newsroom https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-08-11/ai-persona-badges-transparency/ ;
  TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/spotify-will-label-ai-persona-profiles-and-exclude-their-music-from-recommendations/ ;
  NPR https://www.npr.org/2026/08/13/nx-s1-5929744/spotify-labeling-ai-personas
- UMG AI covers/remixes: Spotify Newsroom https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-05-21/universal-music-group-spotify-licensing-agreements-fan-made-covers-remixes/ ;
  TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/21/spotify-and-universal-music-strike-deal-allowing-fan-made-ai-covers-and-remixes/ ;
  Forbes https://www.forbes.com/sites/maureenkerr/2026/05/22/spotify-and-universal-just-put-ai-music-behind-a-paywall/

**Irony / insight.** By Spotify's own definition the line is not "no AI music". AI music
gets demoted when it pretends to be a person, and sold as a premium extra when it borrows a
real one.

**Last line.** "On Spotify, a fake singer gets a badge. A fake version of a real singer gets a price."

**Image.** Two microphones. One gets a warning sticker and is wheeled into a cupboard. The
other stands on a velvet plinth with a price tag swinging from it.

**Risks.**
- Nuance carries the joke. On Spotify's own terms the two policies are consistent
  (transparency about identity), so do not claim Spotify "bans AI music".
- No confirmed report yet that badges went live or of when the UMG tool launches. Both are
  announcements.
- A Spotify boycott planned for 26–27 Oct appeared in one blog only. **Do not use.**

## F1. Fair-game follow-up to the excluded Finland story (new after 6 Oct)

On **11 Oct 2026** Google replied to LVV that preparatory work at Muhos and Kajaani has
stopped. It reserved the right to continue "stabilisation work" with LVV's permission. It
also said it began the forestry work on its own understanding of the law, considers the
authorities were aware of it, and had contacted building control and the Finnish Forest
Centre beforehand. LVV's own order records that the authority had considered the Muhos
landscape-works permit should not be granted while the EIA was incomplete, and that the
municipality did not grant it.
- Yle https://yle.fi/a/74-20251056 ; Kaleva https://www.kaleva.fi/google-vastasi-viranomaiselle-datakeskustyot-keske/13706132 ;
  LVV https://lvv.fi/-/lupa-ja-valvontavirasto-kehottaa-tuike-finland-oy-ta-keskeyttamaan-muhoksen-ja-kajaanin-datakeskushankkeiden-valmistelutoimet
- Possible tag: "Doodle says the authorities knew. They did. They said no to the permit."
- Risk: what each authority knew, and when, is not established. The "advised in May" timing
  comes from one secondary source citing Yle. Present Google's account as its own.
