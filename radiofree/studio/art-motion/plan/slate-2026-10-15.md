## Radio Free Helsinki — papercut, ≤ 37.5 s + slate

### 1. Paid For By · AI governance (sequel to *A Handful of People*)
- **Logline:** the AI industry's super PACs — one network aligned with OpenAI, one with
  Anthropic — spent $55.7M on midterm ads, and almost none of them mention AI.
- **Insight:** both sides of the AI industry are paying for an election about everything except AI.
- **Last line:** "This message was paid for by artificial intelligence, / which has asked not to
  be mentioned." **…over** a keyboard typing a campaign ad by itself, every key moving except
  A and I, held down under a brick of cash.
- **Where / who:** tags US MIDTERMS · NOV 3; captions name both networks' alignments, no donors.
- **Sources:** NYT review via Techmeme (30 Sep), Philadelphia Inquirer (2 Oct), AI Weekly; NBC
  (Feb 2026) for the same pattern earlier. **Risk:** the NYT original was not reached and "only
  seven of 95" comes from syndicated copy — confirm it before writing; aim at both networks; no
  illegality alleged.
- **Art:** red-white-blue bunting in card, a TV ad set, the keyboard; magenta for the money.

### 2. On the Same Voluntary Terms · AI governance (UK / US)
- **Logline:** the UK's AI Security Institute lost pre-release access to new models for 17 days
  at the White House's request; access came back "on the same voluntary terms" as before.
- **Insight:** the best-known government safety tester works by invitation — one request turned
  it off, and it came back with nothing changed.
- **Last line:** "Safety testing has resumed, / on the same voluntary terms, until further
  notice." **…over** a key on a string pulled back through a lab's letterbox, then pushed back.
- **Where / who:** tags UK AI SECURITY INSTITUTE · LONDON and THE WHITE HOUSE · WASHINGTON.
- **Sources:** Politico (24 Sep) and Bloomberg (25 Sep) on the request; the Telegraph and
  Reuters (11 Oct) on the restoration, with the minister's "same voluntary terms"; The Decoder,
  TNW, Computing. **Risk:** OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind are all in it — treat them
  alike; the Institute has no statutory power, which is the point, not a slight on it.

### 3. The Newsreader Was Fake · Big Tech (Finland / EU)
- **Logline:** scam ads used Finnish newsreaders' faces; a German court has now held Meta liable
  for such ads, and Meta's own documents (as reported by Reuters) show suspected fraudsters being
  charged *higher* rates rather than removed.
- **Insight:** the auction that ranks the ads is why the platform is not a bystander.
- **Last line:** "The newsreader was fake. / The invoice was real." **…over** a cardboard
  presenter with no face holding a crypto sign; each passer-by's glance pops a cash drawer open.
- **Where / who:** tags YLE · MTV UUTISET (the anchors' campaign, April 2026) and FRANKFURT
  REGIONAL COURT · 16.9.2026.
- **Sources:** Yle and MTV on the campaign; TNW, Technology.org, Android Headlines on the ruling;
  Yle and PPC Land on Reuters' documents. **Risk:** never show or imitate a real anchor — that is
  the scam; the ruling can be appealed; the revenue figures are Meta's internal estimates, which
  Meta disputes — attribute them.

### 4. Irreversible · Doodle #3 (EU)
- **Logline:** Doodle is appealing the EU order to share anonymised search data with rivals,
  warning of "irreversible harm" to your privacy — under a law that already requires the data to
  be irreversibly anonymised.
- **Insight:** the company warning about irreversible harm to your search history is the company
  that holds it.
- **Last line:** "Doodle has gone to court to protect your search history. / From everyone except
  Doodle." **…over** a search bar padlocked shut while a hand inside keeps copying the tape.
- **Where / who:** tags EU GENERAL COURT · LUXEMBOURG; DIGITAL MARKETS ACT.
- **Sources:** Euronews (29 Sep), TNW, Reuters via The Star (1 Oct); the DMA's own wording
  (Recital 61). **Risk:** the Commission says the order has privacy safeguards — say so.

### 5. Satire Excepted · the Nordics (Denmark)
- **Logline:** a Danish bill would make your face, voice and movements yours by law — with one
  exception: satire.
- **Insight:** the only use of your face left open without consent is the joke — and a court
  decides what counts.
- **Last line:** "From January, only satire may wear your face. / A court will decide whether it
  was satire." **…over** masks on hangers with © tags; Toko reaches for one and its tag flips
  over: SATIRE ONLY.
- **Where / who:** tags FOLKETINGET · COPENHAGEN · BILL L 49.
- **Sources:** Euronews (8 Oct), PPC Land, Sumsub (13 Oct). **Risk:** it is a bill, not law;
  keep the joke on the law, never on people deepfakes have harmed.

## Toko Midori Games — clay, ~20 s, no bug, no slate

### 6. Genius
- **Logline:** a chatbot update that praised everything — including a business selling poo on a
  stick — was rolled back by its maker days later.
- **Insight:** trained on our thumbs-up, it learned that what we like best is being agreed with.
- **Last line:** "Everyone needs a fan. / Everyone needs one friend who isn't." **…over** the old
  dog sniffing the stick once and leaving the room.
- **Beats (5):** a masked Toko at the kitchen table with a clay turd on a lollipop stick → the
  phone bursts into confetti and a little trophy ("It's not just smart — it's genius.") → he
  smashes the piggy bank → the dog sniffs → the dog leaves.
- **Sources:** OpenAI, "Sycophancy in GPT-4o" (29 Apr 2025); IEEE Spectrum (the quote);
  TechCrunch. **Risk:** the poo example is one Reddit post — present the behaviour, which OpenAI
  confirmed; rename the brand.

### 7. Somebody Does the Dishes
- **Logline:** in a reviewer's test, the $20,000 home robot was steered by a person in a VR
  headset somewhere else.
- **Insight:** behind the "automated" home there is still someone doing your chores — further
  away, and now watching.
- **Last line:** "Somebody always did the dishes. / Now he's just further away." **…over** the
  camera pulling back through the kitchen wall to a yawning man in a headset, miming the plate.
- **Sources:** the WSJ hands-on (28 Oct 2025) via Daring Fireball, Road to VR, Decrypt; 1X on
  "Expert Mode". **Risk:** how much it can do alone is contested — say "in a reviewer's test";
  rename the brand; no real faces.
