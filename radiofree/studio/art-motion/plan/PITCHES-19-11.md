# RFH pitches, Thu 19 Nov

Owner, 19 Nov: "Pitch me top RFH videos". Ranked. Sources are the scout entries of 17–19 Nov (GLOBAL-SCOUT, HS-SCOUT, RFH-NEXT).

## A. Who Wrote the Bill? (Coxon; Tucker County / Davis, West Virginia) — recommended
- **Record:** HB 2014, Power Generation and Consumption Act, signed 30 Apr 2025; bars local rules that "limit, in any way, the creation of" microgrid districts or data-centre projects (as quoted by WBOY). Davis faces a 10,000-acre Fundamental Data campus. On a July recording, Fundamental Data's Ted McGavran: "we're the guys that wrote the bill and got it through the Legislature" (Gray TV, 13 Nov). Speaker Hanshaw's office denies it; the governor's office disputes it.
- **Device:** the town's rulebook. A clerk types a line; it is stamped with the law's own words. The last page is the bill's header with the recorded line pinned to it, and the denials beside it.
- **Before building:** the bill's final text; the Gray TV piece itself; a second source for "the sponsor is the company's lawyer" (else it stays out); any company response.

## B. Not Yet Listed (Helsinki; HE 282/2026, the data-centre register)
- **Record:** the government's bill would create Finland's first register of data centres operating or planned (uses, ownership, electricity, grid effects), from 1 Apr 2027 if passed (Edilex; valtioneuvosto). Until then there is no national list.
- **Device:** the blank map. Our own Finnish data-centre stories (Hamina, Muhos, Jokela, Lapinlahti) pinned by hand on a map of Finland, each with its newspaper source, under a register heading that says FROM 1 APR 2027. The series' own index, made into a film.
- **Before building:** open the bill's text on Edilex (threshold, what is public vs. confidential).

## C. Due 13 Nov (Coxon; the House letter to OpenAI)
- **Record:** 20 House Democrats (Rep. Casar) wrote to Sam Altman on 29 Oct about three fired safety researchers: 11 sets of questions, answers due 13 Nov; also asks OpenAI to keep its pause on its most capable models until there is independent oversight. No public answer found as of 18 Nov. OpenAI: the firings followed an internal investigation into a breach of its rules on sensitive information; the researchers dispute that (as reported).
- **Device:** the read receipt. The letter delivered, a "seen" tick, a due date passing on the calendar, eleven numbered questions with empty answer lines.
- **Before building:** the letter on Casar's site; whether OpenAI answered privately or publicly; name the researchers only if their own 8 Oct open letter confirms they went public.
