# Irreversible (`irr`): sound events for the composer

The film runs 37.5 s at 112 BPM (one beat = 0.536 s).
- Cuts fall at 6.43, 12.86, 19.29, 25.71 and 32.14. Each is a 0.5 s dissolve starting on the cut.
- All times are absolute seconds in the picture, taken from the scene code (`scenes/irr_*.js`).

Music, from the shot table: a glossy keynote groove (marimba, pluck, D major). It turns into
courtroom pizzicato strings when the case begins at the cut to shot 2 (6.43). The table says
140 BPM, but the picture is cut on the 112 grid. If you keep 140, line up only the hits below,
not the bars. The last hit is the padlock click at 32.73. After it the groove should thin out to
pencil and tape, so the last line (34.74) lands almost dry. Let the tail ring out to 37.50 and
never chop it.

Two textures run under several shots:
- **The tape:** a quiet ticker or teleprinter chatter, paper running through rollers. It plays in
  shots 1, 4 and 6.
- **The cursor:** shot 1 only. It blinks at about 2.2 Hz, so add a tick if you want one.

## Shot 1 · 0.00–6.43 · Brussels: the order
- **0.05–0.45** The EU emblem (a blue disc) pops up in the sky. Use a soft card *pop*.
- **The stars:** the twelve gold stars light one at a time, clockwise from the top. Give each a
  rising glock or twinkle at 0.25 · 0.34 · 0.43 · 0.52 · 0.61 · 0.70 · 0.79 · 0.88 · 0.97 · 1.06 ·
  1.15 · 1.24.
- **1.24–2.84** A warm glow swells over the full ring and fades.
- **1.55** An envelope drops out of the ring. It flutters and swings down for about 1.4 s (paper
  swish).
- **2.95** The envelope lands on the Doodle search bar. This is a paper slap with a small thud,
  and the bar wobbles for about 0.3 s after it. The envelope reads SHARE DATA.
- **0.50** The tag EUROPEAN COMMISSION · DIGITAL MARKETS ACT appears. It needs no sound.
- The paper tape of searches runs through the bar and down into Doodle's cabinet for the whole
  shot. This is the tape texture.

## Shot 2 · 6.43–12.86 · Luxembourg: the court (pizzicato from here)
- **The steps:** Doodle's briefcase hops up nine steps, one hop per eighth note. Each landing is a
  small clunk, with pizzicato steps climbing in pitch, at 7.21 · 7.48 · 7.74 · 8.01 · 8.28 · 8.55
  · 8.82 · 9.08 · 9.35.
- **9.64–10.44** A speech card rises out of the briefcase and unrolls upward (paper zip or scroll),
  reading "IRREVERSIBLE HARM". Put an accent as it settles at about 10.4.
- The EU and Luxembourg flags wave gently and need no sound.

## Shot 3 · 12.86–19.29 · The law book
- **13.21–14.11** The book's cover swings open (a heavy board whoosh) and lands at about 13.9 (soft
  thump).
- **14.11, 14.38, 14.65** Three pages turn (a page-flip riffle, three flicks).
- **15.16–17.76** The camera pushes in on the line *irreversibly altered*. A long rise works here.
- **15.80–16.40** A gold line draws itself under those words (marker squeak, left to right). This
  is the hit of the shot.

## Shot 4 · 19.29–25.71 · Doodle's cabinet
- **19.29–21.99** The camera pulls back to show how huge the cabinet is. Use a low swell, and keep
  the tape texture running: three tapes feed into its top the whole shot.
- **The drawers burst:** nine drawers burst open, one per eighth note. Each is a metallic *chunk* or
  boing with a rattle of paper, and the tape spills out after it. They fall at 22.24 · 22.50 ·
  22.77 · 23.04 · 23.31 · 23.57 · 23.84 · 24.11 · 24.38.

## Shot 5 · 25.71–32.14 · Both sides, on a scale
- **26.37** Doodle's speech card (Doodle colours) falls into the left pan.
- **26.79** It lands (a clunk). The beam tips left, wobbling for about 1 s (brass creak).
- **28.51** The EU's speech card (blue, ring of stars) falls into the right pan.
- **28.93** It lands (a clunk). The beam swings back and hangs level, swaying ever smaller to about
  31.0. A gentle tick-tock works here, because the case is undecided. There is no padlock in this
  shot.

## Shot 6 · 32.14–37.50 · The lock (the joke)
- **32.19–32.62** Doodle's padlock swings in from the lower left (a short whoosh and a jangle).
- **32.73 THE CLICK.** The shackle snaps shut on the bar's hasp, which is the punch of the film.
  Make it a crisp, dry lock click with a small chime. The lock then sways and settles over about
  1 s.
- **32.73–37.50** Through the glass, Doodle's gloved hand scribbles a copy of the tape with a
  pencil. Run a busy pencil scratch the whole time, under the tape chatter. The copy tape runs on
  into Doodle's cabinet.
- **34.54–36.04** The camera pushes in on the hand. The second caption line, "From everyone but
  Doodle.", lands at 34.74, so give it room with a little silence or a single pluck.
- **37.15–37.50** The captions fade out. Let everything ring out to 37.50.
