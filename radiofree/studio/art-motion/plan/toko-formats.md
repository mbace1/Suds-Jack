# Toko films — different settings, different formats, the same message

Owner, 2026-10-15: "Scrap Genius … these look and sound exactly the same. Look into the Toko
persona and plan different settings and formats but with similar messaging."

## Why the Toko films looked and sounded the same

Happy Little Bot, the Deer, Genius and Dishes share every layer: the same clay renderer, grade
and depth of field; a domestic interior (window, lamp, table); Toko seated as a passive onlooker;
five beats with captions at the top; one wry closing line; a cosy waltz or bossa. Different
stories in one costume.

## Who Toko is (the persona, from `toko/BRAND.md` §1–2c and the counter's own lines)

- **Toko Midori, 美鳥十湖, The Game Creator** — a masked artist. "The mask is not a gimmick — it is
  the terms": you get the work, not a face or a founder story.
- **Uses AI out loud, on purpose** — "a tool with a seam in it, and I let the seam show." What he
  will not do is "let the machine be the audience": a world "where everything is generated and
  nothing is made."
- **"I still decide. That is the whole line."** — the tool is not the problem; handing somebody
  else the deciding is.
- **GO MAKE YOUR OWN** · NO PUBLISHER · NO LAUNCHER · NO ACCOUNT.
- **One person, and clusters** — "Toko is also everyone who ever shipped something from here — and
  every player who has not arrived yet. The head is full of smaller heads."
- **Colours change with the room and the mood** (nine carriers, magenta first).
- **Toko Slomo** — "me, in Kallio Noir": grey hair, a mask, a knife behind his back. The owner's own
  valid version.
- **The voice:** short, dry, warm, all-caps, unhurried — "TAKE YOUR TIME. I AM NOT GOING ANYWHERE."

## The same message, every time

Each film is about a person and a machine, and ends on the human side — in **Toko's own voice**:
one closing card in the counter's all-caps register, held ≥ 4 s (owner: the text went out too
fast). The message family: *don't be the audience · you still decide · the people behind the
machine · go make your own.*

## What changes every time

Each Toko film gets a different **format** (its own editing, camera and caption treatment), a
different **setting** (outdoors, public, a studio, the night city), a different **light**, and a
different **sound genre**. Clay stays the base medium (owner: clay first, and keep the clay elements
as they are); two formats below would add a second medium on top and are marked.
