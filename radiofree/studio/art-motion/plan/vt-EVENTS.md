Music brief (from the shot table): a stiff-upper-lip string-quartet minuet, very proper. It stops dead for the
telephone in shot 3 and comes back, unchanged, from shot 4 or 5. In shot 6 it is cut short by the click.

## Shot 1 · the door (0.000–6.429)
- 0.15–2.00 · a black cab passes left to right across the foreground (wet-tyre swish, loudest at **1.07**).
- 0.50 / 0.82 / 1.14 · three boxes start sliding in along the wet flags on their strings (card scrape, 1.3 s each).
- **1.80 / 2.12 / 2.44** · each box stops at the foot of the steps (soft card thud and a small wobble).
- **2.25** · the green door's latch, then it swings open (2.25–2.70). Warm light spills out.
- **3.27 / 3.87 / 4.47** · each box hops up the steps onto the threshold (a hop and a bump).
- 3.82 / 4.42 / 5.02 · each box goes inside, past the masked tester.
- 5.00–5.60 · a glint crosses the brass plate (a small ting at **5.30**).

## Shot 2 · by invitation (6.429–12.857)
- **6.98 / 7.23 / 7.48** · a hand-written tag drops under each box (centre, left, right) and swings (paper flick).
- 8.63 · the left tester sips tea (a cup on a saucer).
- 9.88 · the middle tester leans in toward the middle box.
- **10.38** · the middle box is twitched back on its string, out of reach (string twang, short scrape). The tester flinches.
- 11.43 · the right tester sips tea.

## Shot 3 · the call (12.857–19.286)
- **13.41–14.06** and **14.36–15.01** · the red telephone rings on the far shore (two bursts; a transatlantic ring
  that interrupts the minuet). It shakes, its handset hops, red ring-lines fly off it.
- **15.13** · the first string snaps taut. **15.40** · the second.
- **15.21** · the first box is yanked out of the door; it hits the quay at 15.43 and goes into the water at **15.71**.
- **15.48** · the second box is yanked out; it hits the quay at 15.70 and goes into the water at **15.98**.
- 15.21–18.08 · the two labs' reels wind (ratchet clicks, slowing toward the end).
- 17.81 / 18.08 · the two boxes arrive at the far shore, beside the labs. The third box stays in the doorway.

## Shot 4 · 17 days (19.286–25.714)

- 22.49–23.79 · the camera settles on the 17. Silence or a held chord suits this.

## Shot 5 · back, as before (25.714–32.143)

- 26.21 / 26.53 · two boxes start sliding back in on their strings; they stop at **27.51 / 27.83**.
- **27.96** · the latch, and the door opens (27.96–28.41).
- **28.98 / 29.58** · each box hops up onto the threshold; they go inside at 29.53 / 30.13.
- 30.71–31.31 · a glint on the brass plate (a ting at **31.01**).

## Shot 6 · until the phone rings again (32.143–37.500)
- 32.64 · a little card drops from above. **32.98** · it catches on the hook under the brass plate (a light tap)
  and swings: OPEN · BY INVITATION.
- 33.34–34.04 · a glint on the plate.
- 34.19–35.54 · the camera eases back to the whole water.
- **35.89–36.25** · the red telephone gives ONE small rattle (half a ring, quieter than shot 3).
- 35.95–36.16 · the two strings twitch taut.
- **36.69** · the door clicks shut. This is the last hit. The card swings from the slam.
- 36.69–37.50 · let it ring out. The captions fade from 37.15.
