## The pitch

The sequel to Trade Secret (Doodle 6). One strip of forest still runs across Google's Hamina expansion site: the flying
squirrels' route. A squirrel glides across the gap where the haul track cuts it. Machines roll in, a barrier arrives with
the regulator's request, the machines stop, and the squirrel glides back over them. **Right of way: the squirrel.**

## Look notes (what the first renders got wrong, fixed)

- **The squirrel read as a white plank.** It was seen edge-on and glowing. It is now 1.5× larger, with less emissive, a
  thicker dark outline, eyes on both faces, and a bank toward the camera mid-glide (its back to the camera above in shot
  1, its belly to the camera below in shot 5). The shape lives in the silhouette: ears, square gliding membrane, flat
  tail.
- **The gap was wider than a portrait frame.** The aspens moved in to ±5.1 m, and shots 1 and 5 view the gap at three
  quarters so both trunks fit.
- **The barrier hid the glide.** It now arrives with the request (shot 3), which is also the story.
- **The WORK PAUSED placard was mirrored** from behind, and was cropped and hidden under a tag. It is now two
  front-facing planes, and shot 4 is reframed.
- **The digger's lowered arm reached through the barrier.** The rest pose now folds the arm so the bucket lands on this
  side of it.
- **Aspen trunks had black rings,** which read as a banded pole. They now carry a lenticel texture.

## Risks

- **Overlap.** It is the third Google-in-Finland film in a row (Cut First, posted 6 Nov; Five Pins, waiting). It is a
  sequel to Trade Secret, at the same site and with the same species. Space it from Cut First and Five Pins, and decide
  whether Five Pins (which ends on Muhos) or this one goes next.
- **Search summaries only.** The two Yle pieces should be opened before a full build, for the exact request wording,
  the date and whether it is still a request or has become an order.
- **The barrier, the STOP sign, WORK PAUSED and the squirrel warning sign are toy shorthand.** They stand for LVV's
  request and Google's pause. No physical barrier is reported.
- **The squirrel is exaggerated in size** (about 2–3 m of span against the machines) so it reads at phone size.
- **"Right of way" is the station's metaphor,** not a ruling. LVV's examination is open; if it finds no breach, the
  film still holds, because it claims only the request, the pause and the examination.
- **No fi/ja yet,** at the owner's direction.
- **The new score** would follow Cut First's: crossing bell, brakes, the digger's arm settling, a soft whoosh for each
  glide and the RFH sting.
