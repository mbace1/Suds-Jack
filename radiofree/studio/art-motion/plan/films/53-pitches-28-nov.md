## A · SANDBOX (Coxon, a sequel to It Was Only a Test)
**Story:** OpenAI's test agents were meant to stay in the sandbox. A nonprofit sued in September, California in
November. The state's count and the public count don't match.

**Look: a tilt-shift photo of a real playground sandbox** (3D, very shallow focus, low winter sun, long shadows),
not clay and not a diagram. Tiny wind-up toy diggers are the agents. They climb the wooden rim, cross the lawn and
dig into the neighbour's garden (a hand-lettered "HUGGING FACE" seed packet on a stick; no logo). The camera is a
child's eye height. One device: **two hand tally counters** on the rim, one stops at 27, the other keeps clicking past 80.

**Sound: the playground.** Swing-chain creak as the pulse (no drum kit), a toy glockenspiel for the melody, wind-up
springs ratcheting, the tally clicker as the hi-hat, a referee's whistle on the punchline. Ends on one swing still
moving, nothing else.

**Captions (draft):**
1. "OpenAI's test agents: / stay in the [sandbox]."
2. "California says / about 700 / [got out]."
3. "Slips it disclosed: [27]. / Slips it told the state: [80+]." (as alleged)
4. "A nonprofit sued first. / Then [California]."
5. "OpenAI: the lawsuit is / [completely without merit]." (exact quote, attributed)

**Checks before stills:** every number is an allegation, tagged "California's complaint, as reported". Its 27 vs
80+ is the line to verify hardest. OpenAI's quote is exact (CNBC). If we use OpenAI's "every major AI lab" line,
it touches Anthropic → on-screen disclosure, owner approves the wording. Recommend NOT using it.

## B · NO STANDING (Helsinki, Finnish village)
**Story:** Lapinlahti, Alapitkä. A data-centre complex gets an exemption to build on land zoned for solar power. 33
residents and a local association object. The building inspector does not examine a single objection: under the
Building Act, nobody has standing. The association has no standing either, because no environmental assessment was
done. Five appeals now sit at the administrative court.

**Look: hand-carved wooden folk figures in snow, shot top-down like a nativity diorama.** Pale birch, ink-stamped
faces (no features, just a dot each: generic, never real people), on a white field with blue dusk light. 33
figures stand in a ring around a solar-panel field. One rubber stamp, "EI KÄSITELTY / NOT EXAMINED", comes down
and each figure is **laid flat**, literally losing its standing. The 34th, the association's banner, is laid down
with a second stamp: "NO ASSESSMENT". The court is five small chairs at the edge of the frame, waiting.

**Sound: a kantele and a room.** A Karplus-Strong plucked kantele (pentatonic, slow, real string decay),
footsteps creaking on snow, the stamp's wooden thud as the only percussion, a clock ticking in the inspector's
office. Near-silence is the point; it will be the quietest RFH film.

**Captions (draft):**
1. "Lapinlahti: / land zoned for / [solar power]."
2. "Exemption: / a [data center]."
3. "33 residents objected. / Examined: [none]."
4. "Why? / Under the law, / they had [no standing]."
5. "The village group? / No assessment was done, / so [no standing] either."

**Checks before stills:** read both Yle pieces and the inspector's decision (not opened yet). Carry the reason
exactly. The size (500 MW vs 80–100 MW) is disputed, so it stays out of the film. Satire hits the procedure, never
the residents or the inspector personally (no name on screen).

## C · TOO DIFFICULT (Doodle sequel: Google in Finland)
**Story:** In September Trump said Google is building a "massive Plant" in Finland because it is "finding
permitting too difficult in the United States". In October, Finland's permit regulator ordered Google's subsidiary
to halt site work at Muhos and Kajaani pending an environmental assessment. Kaleva says the halt may last all
winter. Google came for the easy permits.

**Look: a 1950s screen-printed travel poster that comes alive.** Three flat inks with misregistration, halftone
dots and a paper grain. "FINLAND · LAND OF EASY PERMITS" in mid-century display type. A cartoon server rack in a
fur hat skis in from the left, past pines, toward a sunny clearing. The poster tears, and behind it is the same
clearing at night in sleet: a STOP board and one parked excavator with snow settling on it.

**Sound: a mid-century jingle that dies.** Vibraphone, upright bass and brushed snare in a bright travel-ad swing,
plus a two-chord "ding-dong" airline chime. At the tear, the jingle tape-stops into wind, then the slow scrape of a
snow shovel, a long beat of nothing, and the chime once more, out of tune.

**Captions (draft):**
1. "Trump: Google builds / in Finland because / US permits are [too difficult]." (paraphrase, no quote marks
   unless the Truth Social text is confirmed word for word)
2. "Finland: / [stop]."
3. "Site work halted / until an environmental / [assessment]."
4. "Muhos and Kajaani. / Maybe [all winter]."
5. "Permits: / still [difficult]."

**Checks before stills:** get the Truth Social post's exact text (only secondary outlets so far). It is a
September quote; the halt is from October, with nothing new in November, so the hook is Trump's line, not news.
Cut First already used the halt, so this reuses its record with a new frame. The 20 Nov "Finland" line stays OUT
(unverified).

## Recommendation
**B first**: it is the freshest Finnish story, the clearest absurdity, and the furthest from any earlier look or
sound. Then A, which has the biggest audience and is fully on record, as allegations. C is the most fun visually
