## The story: *Eight in Ten* (research: `plan/research-japan.md` §1, CONFIRMED)

- **The target.** Japan's AI Basic Plan, in its reported draft (Jiji, Dec 2025), aimed for **80%** of people to use
  generative AI. The final text still needs checking for "8割".
- **Where use stands.** The 2026 white paper (MIC, 24 Jul) puts use at **58.8%**: 78% of people in their 20s, 34% of
  those in their 60s.
- **The push.** On 1 Oct the PM ordered an AI action plan by the end of the year.
- **The gap in the count.** The survey was done **online**, so the farmhouse was never asked.

**The one question:** the government wants eight in ten of us using AI. Who are the other two?

It is a **twin film**, the format the personas were made for. The same news reaches both places on the same morning:
- In Tokyo it lands on three screens at once.
- In Kyoto it comes over the transistor radio, and he pours the tea.
- Kyoto Toko declined by choice, not out of inability. That is *you still decide*.

**Last line (Kyoto Toko's card):** EIGHT IN TEN. THE OTHER TWO ARE HAVING TEA.
The Japanese draft is 「十人に八人。残りの二人は、お茶を飲んでいる。」 and needs a native read.

## Two ways to make it (the owner's call)

1. **Clay, the original style.** One Toko per room: the irori and the 1K flat from `clay/japan/`. Cut between them
   with no crowd and no product shots. This answers the owner's note of 19 Oct most directly.
2. **The 2D tests:** woodblock for Kyoto, manga or JRPG for Tokyo, side by side. This is the bolder look, and it ships
   two of the 17 Oct tests inside a real film.
