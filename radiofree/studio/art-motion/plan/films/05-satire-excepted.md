# 5 · Satire Excepted — Radio Free Helsinki · the Nordics · papercut · 37.5 s + slate

**Logline:** a Danish bill would make your face, voice and movements yours by law — with one exception: satire.
**Insight:** the only use of your face left open without consent is the joke, and a court decides what is a joke.
**The joke at the end:** the station's own presenter — Toko, masked — takes a mask off a hanger tagged SATIRE ONLY and puts it on: we're fine, we're only joking.

**Colour (its own):** Copenhagen — Danish red `#c8102e` and white, warm candle amber, pale beech wood, a copper-green spire; cosy, not autumn-dark.
**Music (its own):** a jaunty accordion-and-harpsichord waltz (3/4 on the 168 grid), with a little *ta-da* for the last line.

**Sources:** Euronews (8 Oct 2026: Denmark tables a bill protecting faces and voices from deepfakes); PPC Land; Sumsub (13 Oct); EPRS note. Bill L 49, tabled 8 Oct by the culture minister; entry into force aimed at 1 Jan 2027; satire, parody, caricature and criticism stay allowed unless the fake is disinformation that seriously endangers others' rights; courts decide case by case.
**Risks / fairness:** it is a bill, not law — said in shot 3. The joke is on the law and on ourselves, never on people deepfakes have harmed. No minister named.
