**The one question:** a home screen that knows your face. **What does it keep when you leave?**
**The answer:** your data, it says it forgets. Your face: nobody can see it, and how long it's kept isn't said. And
its own ad ends with the family watching a guest leave on its camera. **And your guests?** A question by design:
how a visitor's face is handled is undisclosed, not shown to be kept.
