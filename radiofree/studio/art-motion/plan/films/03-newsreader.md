# 3 · The Newsreader Was Fake — Radio Free Helsinki · Big Tech · papercut · 37.5 s + slate

**Logline:** scam ads wore Finnish newsreaders' faces; Meta's own papers, as Reuters reported, charged suspected scammers more instead of removing them; a German court has now held Meta liable for such ads.
**Insight:** the auction that sells the ad space is why the platform is not a bystander.
**The joke at the end:** the cardboard newsreader falls flat on its face; behind it a cash register keeps printing a receipt that runs out of the frame.
