# Guest in Her Own Kitchen (the Welcome Home sequel): pitch, 19 Oct

This is backlog pick 3: the owner's "Pear sequel, *Remote Control for an iPhone*". The research was refreshed on 19 Oct
(`plan/research-pear-sequel.md`) and **it changed the premise**.

- **Why the old premise had to go.** "Remote control for an iPhone" is not fair as stated. HomeView has its own chip,
  apps and assistant, and it runs timers, music and the house without any phone. The phrase itself rests on two
  AI-written podcast summaries; nobody has heard it in the episode. It must not appear on screen or under a name.
- **What the facts do support (CONFIRMED).** HomeView recognises a face only if all of these hold:
  - that person's own phone (or iPad) is on the home Wi-Fi;
  - the phone was unlocked recently ("recently" is never defined);
  - the phone runs Pear's AI, which means an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
- **What that means in the kitchen.** Anyone without such a phone gets the guest layer: timers, weather and music,
  but no name, messages or calendar. A grandmother with an older phone lives there and is a guest every morning.
- **The 28 Oct hook is gone.** Apple announced the 28 Oct event ("Pro and tell") today. It is about Macs and an iPad
  mini, and nobody expects a home product. The new hook is the **ship date, 13 Nov**.

**The one question:** does Pear's home screen know the family, or their phones?

**Rules and risks**
- **Brands.** Pear and HomeView, as in Welcome Home; "your phone", never iPhone. No Siri; Pear's AI is unnamed.
- **Quotes.** None. Nilay Patel's line and Apple's footnotes stay off screen.
- **Accuracy.**
  - An iPad also counts, so the captions say "phone" generically.
  - The phone can stay in a pocket.
  - Do not imply HomeView stores your messages, is sold in Finland, or has had a recall.
- **The EU twist,** held for a tag line if wanted: it is not sold in Finland at all, so here everybody is a guest.
- **Look.** Welcome Home's set and family, with one new puppet (Grandma), so it is mostly re-staging. About 20 s.
