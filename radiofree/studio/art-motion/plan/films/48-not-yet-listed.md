# 48 · Not Yet Listed (Helsinki 4; HE 282/2026, the data-centre register)

Owner, 19 Nov: "Top3". Facts from search snippets (19 Nov); **no line of the bill has been read on the page** (edilex.fi / eduskunta.fi / valtioneuvosto.fi / tem.fi are blocked here).

## The record
- **HE 282/2026 vp**, a law on a data centre register (laki datakeskusrekisteristä); submitted 29 Oct 2026 (TEM/valtioneuvosto release "Government proposes new data centre register to improve situational awareness"; Dawn.fi 30 Oct); TEM's bill; in force 1 Apr 2027 if passed.
- **Who must notify:** data centres with installed IT power of at least 0.5 MW; smaller ones voluntarily. Within 30 days of a grid-connection agreement and before construction; existing ones by 30 Jun 2027. Reported content: ownership, location, purpose, capacity, schedule, electricity and water use.
- **Keeper:** Huoltovarmuuskeskus (National Emergency Supply Agency), funded by notification fees. Not a permit: no power to inspect or stop a project.
- **Public?** The register itself would be a **non-public situational picture**; HVK would publish a regular (reportedly quarterly) summary of industry and regional development, the first by 30 Sep 2027. Energiateollisuus pressed for more secrecy protection (statement, 2 Oct 2026). **This is the film:** Finland will list its data centres, and you won't see the list.
- **Do NOT say "Finland has no list":** an unofficial site (datakeskusrekisteri.fi, operator unknown) already lists projects; no official sentence found saying no list exists. Minister Ranne told Yle 100–200 projects are "pinnan alla" (Kaleva headline). Yle: end users of several planned centres unknown ("Yhtiön oma asia", a land-use director).
- **Our own pins** (status Nov 2026): Hamina (Google, operating since 2011; expansion in the €13bn package, Sep 2026); Muhos (Google/Tuike; ~330 ha felled without YVA reported; LVV ordered work paused; Google disputes — MT, Kaleva, Yle); Jokela/Tuusula (QTS; building-permit application 16 Nov 2026, 96 MW, 16.7 ha; Tuusula release, Keski-Uusimaa); Lapinlahti (Helios Nordic Energy 330–500 MW and Greensky Data 50–100+ MW, planning stage); Kajaani (LUMI-AI under construction; Google planned).

## Device (draft): the map that goes dark
A paper map of Finland on a corkboard. Hands pin our stories one by one, each pin with its newspaper clipping (Hamina, Muhos, Jokela, Lapinlahti, Kajaani). Then the register arrives: an official binder, REKISTERI / REGISTER, FROM 1 APR 2027, ≥ 0.5 MW. It swallows the pins one at a time; each comes back as a grey stamp, NOT PUBLIC. What is left on the board: a single summary sheet, FIRST SUMMARY 30 SEP 2027.
