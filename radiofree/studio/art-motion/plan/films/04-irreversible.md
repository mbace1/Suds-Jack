# 4 · Irreversible — Radio Free Helsinki · Doodle series #3 · papercut · 37.5 s + slate

**Logline:** the EU ordered Doodle to share anonymised search data with rivals; Doodle went to court warning of "irreversible harm" to privacy — under a law that already requires the data to be irreversibly anonymised.
**Insight:** the company warning about irreversible harm to your search history is the company that keeps it.
**The joke at the end:** a padlock snaps onto the search bar, facing out — and inside, Doodle's own hand keeps copying the tape.

**Colour (its own):** Brussels daylight — EU blue `#1d3f8f`, star gold `#f4c430`, white marble, and the four Doodle colours on its wordmark only; bright and clean, unlike the dark Finnish Doodle films.
**Music (its own):** a glossy keynote groove (marimba, pluck, 140 BPM, D major) that turns into courtroom pizzicato strings when the case begins.
