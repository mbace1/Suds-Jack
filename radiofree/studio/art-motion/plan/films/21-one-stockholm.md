**The one question:** *Doodle wants a Stockholm's worth of power. How many jobs does Sweden get?*

**The answer, in one sentence (copy.json `argument`):** Doodle's planned Swedish data centre may draw up to 2,000 MW,
about all of Stockholm. Jobs: the seller says 500; Doodle, too early to say. Without new power, the grid operator
warns, prices rise nationwide.

## Palette: an October night in Sweden, in card
Night blue in hard cut bands (`#05080f` → `#203253`) with a cream moon; Stockholm in slate card (`#252e40`,
`#1c2331`) with City Hall in dark brick (`#3a2a2c`), copper-green roofs and three gold crowns; the windows warm
(`#ffd27a`), the only warm light in the film. Scots pines: dark green crowns only at the top, rust trunks. The box is
cold grey (`#7d848c`), the only thing in the forest that is not natural. Signs: plywood with red paint; Doodle's white
with its six-colour wordmark. The map: cream land on a dark sea, ochre tags. The label: white card, the familiar seven
coloured bars. Kept apart from *The Biodiversity Fund* and *On Hold*: no spruce, no dusk ochre, no hoarding or cheque.

## The motion technique that carries the twist
**TURN** (`motion-options.md`): shapes that turn into other shapes. On the pop the paper Stockholm folds flat on its
crease, ends first, and the windowless box unfolds up out of the same crease, so the comparison in the caption is one
object becoming another. Everything is a pure function of the shot's time: the fold order and the box's pop-up are
scheduled from the pop cue, so it lands on "all of Stockholm" in every language.

## Risks and how the film handles them
- **"Up to".** 2,000 MW is the contract's ceiling; Doodle says the talks cover up to 1 GW. The caption says "allows up
  to", the tag says UP TO. The Stockholm comparison is the outlets', and the film says "about".
- **The jobs.** The 500 is labelled the seller's, on the sign itself. Doodle's sign says maybe hundreds: the film
  never says Doodle gave no number and never says Doodle denied the 500.
- **The price warning is conditional** ("unless new power is built") and is the grid operator's, named in the tag.
- **The label.** The seven bars are the shape every appliance label has. The arrow points at the longest bar because
  this is the biggest number; there is no efficiency letter, and the film makes no efficiency claim.
- **Doodle**, never Google, and the wordmark is the house's parody one. No real person appears; nobody is named.
- **Keep it Swedish** (not a third Muhos film): the map and the city are Swedish, the strand's Finnish data-centre
  stories are not referenced.
