## What the photos prove, and what they don't

They prove that a weathered 5 L **Roundup Piha** jug (glyphosate, the consumer product) stood in the flowerbed by the Yle
signpost (Mediatalo / Luova talo / Studiotalo) and under the tree by the rock on the morning of 7 Nov 2026. They do not
prove who left it, whether it is empty, whether anything was sprayed there, or whose land the bed is. **The film says
FOUND, never USED**, and attaches nothing to Yle, the City of Helsinki or any contractor beyond "in front of". Photo 5
has a distant passer-by by the railing (top right); every crop of it stays in its lower-left two thirds. The owner's own
photos: no rights question.

## The device: the found object (picked), and the two not picked

- **The found object (picked).** A crime scene that isn't: a slow, quiet walk around the jug, and the only questions the
  photos can honestly raise — who left it (unknown), who allowed it (the EU, until 2033), and what the label says and
  doesn't (its own claim; no cancer warning, because ECHA found no grounds for one). Truest to the record, because it
  claims nothing the pictures can't carry; most surprising, because the thing with the permit is the chemical.
- **The object speaks** (the label's marketing claims as every caption): folded in as beat 2 only; five claims in a
  row would be an ad, not a record.
- **Same label** (the Helsinki jug's empty warning box beside the US one): folded into the turn ("here too"); the US
  story is On the Label's, and a split screen would spend the film explaining Durnell.
