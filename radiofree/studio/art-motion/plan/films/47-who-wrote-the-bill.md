## The record
- **HB 2014 (2025), the Power Generation and Consumption Act**, Enrolled Committee Substitute; signed by Gov. Morrisey 30 Apr 2025 at Point Pleasant (governor's release; WV MetroNews 1 May 2025). "The Governor's bill" (governor's office). Effective 11 Jul 2025 (one secondary source).
- **Preemption, §5B-2-21b (subsection letter unconfirmed)**, as reconstructed from WVCBP / datacenterdata.com snippets: certified microgrid districts and certified high impact data centers "may not be subject to … County or municipal zoning, horticultural, noise, viewshed, lighting, development, or land use ordinances, restrictions, limitations, or approvals". Also building permits/inspection, licensing, and local jurisdiction. 2026: HB 5168 reportedly added that the Legislature will "occupy the whole field"; three 2026 bills to restore local power failed.
- **The line:** Ted McGavran (described as a Fundamental Data engineer/consultant), at a Belmont, NC City Council meeting, 6 Jul 2026, where he lives; he also advised Belmont to adopt a noise ordinance. Gray TV (13 Nov 2026): "Up in West Virginia, we're the guys that wrote the bill and got it through the Legislature." and "We can do pretty much whatever we want to do up there." Country Roads News (21 Jul 2026) headline: "We Were the Guys That Basically Wrote the Bill". **Wording differs ("basically") — take it from the recording.** Gray calls it a "hot mic"; it was a public council meeting.
- **Responses:** Fundamental Data: "Fundamental Data did not draft, write or play any role in drafting HB 2014", and McGavran "is not authorized to speak on behalf of the company" (WVHC, WV Watch, Jul 2026). Governor's office (Lars Dalseide, Jul 2026, one snippet): "HB 2014 was the Governor's bill, and the drafting was deliberately limited to the Governor's office." Morrisey (Gray, 13 Nov) denied the claim.
- **Hanshaw:** Speaker Roger Hanshaw, a Bowles Rice partner, is one of three Bowles Rice lawyers for Fundamental Data in its air-permit appeal (filed 16 Mar 2026; Gazette-Mail, Country Roads, WV Record, Hampshire Review, NPR "lead attorney", Gray). NPR (31 Oct): he says he has never been employed by Fundamental Data, and Bowles Rice's role is public record (paraphrase; get the email text). Sponsorship: say "introduced at the governor's request", not "Hanshaw's bill".
- **The project:** "Ridgeline", gas- and diesel-fired plant plus data centre, about a mile from Davis and from Thomas; 291 acres at first, planned to grow to as much as 10,000 (figures vary; say "up to"). Davis Town Council: a 5–0 **resolution** opposing it (a resolution, not an ordinance). Air permit upheld by the Air Quality Board (Feb 2026); the ICA dismissed the appeals for lack of jurisdiction (26 Aug 2026); the groups went toward the Supreme Court; no ruling found.
- **Nuance that must be in the film:** the state Data Economy Office said it had received no certification application from Fundamental Data, so the preemption may not formally apply to Ridgeline yet. The film says what the law does to every town, not that it has been used on Davis.
