Owner, Sun 22 Nov: "How about prepping in the US?", then "Maybe show bunker footage. A bit like fallout vibe".
Look: the mid-century civil-defence era (1950s shelter brochures, the yellow-and-black fallout-shelter sign, canned rations, a round-
cornered TV), warm amber bulbs against teal steel — ORIGINAL; nothing from the Fallout games (no Vault-Tec, no Vault Boy, no
Pip-Boy screen, no cog-shaped vault door, no names or logos from them).

## The record (search summaries, 22 Nov)
- 13 Nov 2026: a nationwide FAA ground stop, more than four hours; the FAA: a technical problem (communication failures at several
  centers); not a cyberattack, per DHS (CNBC, FAA Q&A, Axios). Then outages at Microsoft and at banks incl. JPMorgan Chase (Fortune 18 Nov).
- Prepping up: searches and big-box sales of water, generators, power banks (NewsNation, Fox LA, Axios 19 Nov "a new prepper boom");
  Costco selling out of generators (Fortune 19 Nov).
- Atlas Survival Shelters' owner: one shelter sold in 2018, now about one a day; $20,000 to millions (TribLive) — HIS claim, attributed.
- Vice President JD Vance to Laura Ingraham (Fox News, aired ~16/17 Nov): "We do not see a crisis coming down, but you have to be
  prepared for everything" (Yahoo/Fox; Fortune has "don't"). He said no need to build a bunker or stockpile.

## Device
One set: a corrugated steel shelter tube under a backyard, bunks, shelves, water, a generator, a ladder up to a hatch; the round TV on
the table carries the news. Each beat the shelter fills further; the last shot climbs the ladder and the hatch shuts on the quote.
