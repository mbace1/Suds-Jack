**The one question:** *How do you count a forest that is gone?*

**The answer, in one sentence (copy.json `argument`):** Doodle's Muhos data-centre site was cleared before the required
environmental assessment: over 300 hectares by the regulator's count, about 250,000 trees by the Guardian's estimate.
Citizens gathered over 50,000 names for data-centre rules in days. Doodle plans tree planting across 130 hectares.

**Why it is not a third copy of the Muhos films:** *The Biodiversity Fund* told the felling side-on (spruces tipping
over, a hectare counter, the cheque, the regulator's stamp, the protest, Doodle's "fell short"); *On Hold* was the
electricity tax. This film is new on two counts. It has a new fact, the Guardian's tree count of 22 Oct, and it has a
new idea: everyone counts in the unit that suits them. Its look is new too: the strand's first film from above, the way
the satellite pictures were. It keeps the owner's strand note from *Biodiversity* v2 ("no vote from the people, nor
confidence from locals"): the citizens' count is one of the four.

## Palette: a Finnish forest in October, from above, in card
Spruce in dark greens (`#14291d`–`#1b3726`) with lit star centres, pines rounder and lighter, birches in October yellow
(`#c8962a`–`#ecc664`). The forest floor is near-black green, the bog pale sedge, the road gravel (`#857a66`), the stream
night blue. The clearing is brown soil (`#5b4632`) with darker ruts and slash and pale cream stumps (`#d7c49a`). The
overlays are the only clean colours: the red marker (`#d8402f`), the cream grid and cards, the ochre rings and tags, and
the sapling green (`#7fcf5a`). Kept apart from *The Biodiversity Fund* (side-on, dusk ochre) and *On Hold* (hoarding,
cranes, gate).

## The motion technique that carries the twist
**MARK** (`motion-options.md`, drawn by hand, here in the kit's own code rather than rough.js): the marker draws the
site's boundary and the clearing follows from it. Every count after that is a mark on the same map: the hectare grid,
the ring round each counted stump, the slips, and the sapling rows. Everything is a pure function of the shot's time.
The felling front (`fellAt()`) is mirrored in `score.py`, so each lane's knock lands on its lane.

## Risks and how the film handles them
- **Attribution.** The 250,000 is the Guardian's estimate. It is attributed on screen (tag and clipping) and in the copy,
  and the punchline says "about".
- **"Illegal".** Not said. The regulator's finding is that the work came before the required assessment, and that is what
  the caption says. Doodle says it acted under the Forest Act.
- **The initiative** is about data centres in general (grid conditions and a fee). The caption says "for data-centre
  rules" and does not tie it to the felling.
- **The patch is drawn to scale:** 40 % of the clearing, for 130 of about 330 hectares. The planting is "planned" and
  part of Doodle's landscaping and biodiversity plans. The film does not claim the rest will never be replanted.
- **Doodle**, never Google, with the house's parody wordmark. Tuike Finland and LVV are real. No person appears, and
  nobody is named.
