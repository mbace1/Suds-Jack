## The story (facts only; every claim attributed)
1. 11 Nov: the Russian missile ship Naro-Fominsk is hit near Gogland, about 40 km off the Finnish coast. Ukraine's SBU claims
   the strike; a senior Finnish official told Yle that Finland detected an attack there. The ship sailed to Kronstadt.
2. The Defence Forces: "no immediate military threat to Finland"; the Baltic security situation "remains stable"; no details.
3. 21 Nov: the cargo ship Sparta II, bound for Kaliningrad, on fire in the same gulf. Russia (the Leningrad Region governor)
   says two drones; Estonia's rescue centre got reports of fire and explosions at 09:35. Not independently confirmed (ERR).
4. 21 Nov, 04:15 and 04:20: the Defence Forces restrict the air and the sea of the eastern gulf AGAIN — a precaution, preparing
   to counter possible drones (Yle, MTV, ESS). Ten days earlier the same, after a reported strike on Ust-Luga.
5. The punchline is the official word itself, set against all of the above: "stable".

## The satire and its limits
The target is the official register (calm words, closing skies) — never the ships, the crews or the war's sides. No
casualty claims (one unverified Telegram claim exists; not used). No weapon named. No attribution beyond what each party says.
The restricted zone on the table is drawn as ILLUSTRATIVE (the exact area is not in the reporting we can see).

## Device and look
Two places, cut against each other: the NIGHT SEA off the coast (sleet, swell, a flash behind Gogland, later a ship burning
on the horizon) and a Defence Forces PLOTTING ROOM (the ICEYE cold-war look: black and navy, phosphor-green radar map of the
gulf, a sweep; red markers; a hatched restricted zone; a teleprinter that types the official lines). The lamp flickers on a
distant thud and goes OUT on the last word.

Sound: its own kit, GULF — wind and swell, a sub drone that beats, sonar pings, a far boom carried over water, fire, a
teleprinter, a radio carrier with static, a low string cluster and timpani that swell, a slow heartbeat; all of it cut dead
when the lamp goes out.
