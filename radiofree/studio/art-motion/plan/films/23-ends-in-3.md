**The one question:** *Should a phone need its owner's ID to work?*

**The answer, in one sentence (copy.json `argument`):** Mexico ties every mobile line to its owner's ID number, against
extortion and fraud. Unlinked lines are cut off by last digit, 2.2 million ending in 3, yet a cut-off phone still gets
the earthquake alert.

## Palette: a Mexican street at dusk, in card
A banded dusk from indigo (`#101528`) down to a warm horizon (`#934a44`); far city in violet-grey card; facades in
terracotta `#9c4a32`, ochre `#c98f3a`, teal `#2f6b68`, pink `#b5566a` and blue `#3f5f88`, cream trim, black iron
balconies, lamp-gold windows. The screens are the cold thing: pale mint `#e6f4f2` with black digits; cut off, slate grey
`#36373b`; the alert is red `#e8452c` with a cream icon. The registry is green-grey institutional walls and a walnut
counter; the ID card cream with a blue band. The calendar is cream paper with a red header and steel rings; the marker
and the string are the house red `#d8402f`; the tally is black with cream wheels.

## The motion technique that carries the twist
**MARK** (rough.js 4.6.6, the `ml_mark` technique as Tailgated uses it): the red marker's X over each last digit is the
whole policy drawn as a calendar, and it moves on past the 3 to the 4 and rings the 5, so the film says "this is still
happening". Every mark is drawn along its own path length, a pure function of the shot's time. The street's phones
carry the rest: dark, lit, grey, back, and finally all the same red, which is the joke without a word.

## Risks and how the film handles them
- **Earthquakes are no joke in Mexico.** Only the alert is shown: an icon on a screen and a soft two-tone sound. No
  quake, no damage, no rumble, nobody runs. The satire is aimed at the registry's logic, not at the alert, which the
  regulator was right to keep.
- **The official reason is stated** in the caption itself: extortion and fraud. Many countries register SIM cards. The
  film asks the question; it does not call the registry surveillance.
- **Temporary, not deleted.** Shot 4 says the number is kept and shows a line coming back once linked. Never "deleted".
- **Not every 3 was cut.** One phone showing 3 stays lit (87 % of the 3s were linked by 4 Oct).
- **Dated numbers.** 2.2 million is the CRT's count as of 4 Oct; the tag carries the date and the post the full figure.
- **No carrier, no biometrics, no person.** The phone companies are not named; biometrics are left out (sources
  disagree on whether a biometric CURP is needed). Every figure is a masked Toko; the ID card's photo is a mask.
