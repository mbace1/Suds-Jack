# Backup Mode (Toko Slomo, Kallio Noir): pitch, 19 Oct

The owner picked it from the backlog on 19 Oct (`plan/toko-formats.md` #3, research `plan/research-toko.md` §8). It is
Toko Slomo's first film. Clay, about 22 s, with no slate and no station bug: the Toko films' own rules. The last card is
in Toko's all-caps voice and held for at least 4 s.

**The hook is the anniversary.** On 20 Oct 2025 an Amazon Web Services outage cut off Eight Sleep's cloud-dependent Pod
covers. Eight Sleep markets the Pod as "AI". Owners reported beds stuck tilted, or heating with no way to turn them down,
in the night. About two days later the maker shipped a Bluetooth "Backup Mode", which only offers manual temperature,
the base, and on/off.
- Sources: PCWorld (×2), Tech Critter, Tom's Guide, Yahoo. The CEO's apology is on X, 20 Oct 2025.
- The film airs a year to the week after the outage.

**The one question:** what does your bed need to lie flat? Toko Slomo narrates it hard-boiled, first person, all caps.

**Rules and risks**
- **Rename the brand.** The bed is unbranded on screen, and the maker is "the maker". No logo, and no "Pod".
- **Quotes.** None. The CEO's apology stays out, and nothing is in quote marks.
- **Hedging.** The fault was the outage plus cloud dependence, not an AI decision. The captions say "some beds", not
  "every bed", because how many owners were affected is not documented.
- **No knife.** Toko Slomo's knife from the brand sheet stays out of a bedroom gag.
- **Look.** Noir in clay: a black-and-white grade with one neon colour, rain, and a hard key from the window.
