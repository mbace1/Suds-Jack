## Risks

1. **Date (the main one).** The calendar and the post say "due 5 Nov", and today is 3 Nov.
   - Posting on 4 Nov is fine as written.
   - After 5 Nov the post needs past tense ("was due 5 Nov"), and a follow-up is needed if Tuike filed it, or failed to.
   - The 5–6 Nov check covers this.
2. **The police car is shorthand.** No report has police at the site. The car stands for "the police are
   investigating", and the copy says no more than that.
3. **The ditch is drawn, not surveyed.** The layers and the rust water illustrate LVV's black-shale finding and the
   general geology; they are not measurements from this site. It is a toy-scale exaggeration, wider and deeper than a
   real ditch.
4. **"First" and "last" are the station's words for the order of events.** The clearing came before the water plan the
   regulator demanded. The film does not say that no plan of any kind existed before.
5. Count the Trees (Doodle 5) covered Muhos before. This is the follow-up with the police investigation, not a repeat.
6. The fi and ja copy have had no native read.
