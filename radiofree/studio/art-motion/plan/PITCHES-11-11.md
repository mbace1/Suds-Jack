# Pitches, Wed 11 Nov: three films in the night kit, and one decision

**Owner, Wed 11 Nov:** "Start pitching new videos with cool visuals and interesting stories. Render and approve".

Each pitch has one device, one hero still (`previews/11-11-RFH-Pitch-*.jpg`; all three on
`previews/11-11-RFH-Pitches-stills.jpg`) and a list of what must be re-read before a build. The stills are pitch

## A · Dragged (Fitburg, the cable verdict)

- **Story:** On 5 Nov the Helsinki District Court convicted the cargo ship Fitburg's captain (3 years 6 months) and
  boatswain (2 years 10 months) of aggravated sabotage. The court says the anchor dragged about 80 miles along the
  seabed and cut two Helsinki–Tallinn data cables around New Year 2025/26. Both deny it; the verdict is not final.
  (12 Nov check: at least about 130 km, so about 80 miles, on 31 Dec 2025. The captain will appeal; the boatswain's
  side was still weighing it, and no notice of appeal is reported. So the card says "not final", not "both appeal".)
- **Device: a ticker on one unbroken shot.** The camera rides with the anchor along the seabed while a mile counter
  climbs. The length of the drag is the argument. It ends where the anchor meets the cable.
- **Look:** the seabed: shafts of light through green water, silt, marine snow, a rusted anchor on its chain, the
  cable with its yellow bands, and cut fibers still carrying light.
- **Beats (about 22 s):**
  1. Dark water; the anchor comes down and hits bottom.
  2. It starts to drag. The counter begins: 1 mile.
  3. The furrow runs on and the counter races: 10, 40, 79.
  4. The cable, taut over the fluke. It parts, and the light in its fibers goes out.
  5. A phone, mid video call: "Connection lost."
  6. The verdict card, with "not final" and the appeal.
- **Sound:** an underwater kit: the hull's engine far above, chain, steel groaning on stone, then silence at the cut.
- **Re-read before a build:** the distance figure, the dates, the sentences, the appeal status, and how the cables'
  owner is named. No faces; the verdict is attributed to the court.

## B · Give Way (Highway 9, fighter jets)

- **Story:** In the Air Force exercise Protective Fence 26 (19–27 Nov), 20 Hornets disperse to bases, civilian airports
  and a highway strip, joined by Dutch, French and Swedish jets. The strip is Jännevirta on Highway 9 near Kuopio, and
  parts of the road are closed 18–25 Nov. Finland's new F-35As do not take part.
- **Device: a borrowed format.** The car's sat-nav tells the story, in one shot from the dashboard.
- **Look:** a snowy forest highway at night under the aurora, a road block with amber flashers, runway lights running
  to a point, and a Hornet coming in over the car with its burners lit.
- **Beats (about 20 s):**
  1. Night drive. Sat-nav: "Continue on Highway 9."
  2. "Road closed ahead. Reason: fighter jets."
  3. A Hornet passes over the car and lands beyond the block.
  4. "Also flying: the Netherlands, France, Sweden." (12 Nov check: "landing" on the highway is not supported for
     all three; one trade source bases the French and Swedish jets elsewhere.)
  5. "Not taking part: Finland's new F-35s." (Keep "Finland's": Dutch F-35s do take part.)
  6. "Recalculating. This road is a runway."
- **Sound:** a car-interior kit: heater fan, indicator, the sat-nav's chime (text only, no voice), and the jets passing
  over.
- **Timing:** it is timely for 18–19 Nov, when the road closes and the exercise starts. To post before the road
  closes, the build needs a yes by about Sat 14 Nov.
- **Re-read before a build:** the closure dates, the nations taking part and the F-35 line, from the Air Force's own
  release.

## C · Keep It Quiet (Jokela, the emails)

- **Story:** Aamuposti and Keski-Uusimaa obtained Tuusula's emails with the PR agency Kreab; the papers' headline is
  that the town asked the agency for help keeping the Jokela data center project quiet. The town denies hiding
  anything and says the preliminary agreement required confidentiality.
  - Councilors did not know about the preliminary agreement.
  - Residents have complained to the Chancellor of Justice.
  - The land (about 16.7 ha) sells to funds managed by Blackstone for €19.2M.
  - The plan is 96 MW with 76 diesel backup generators.
- **Device: caption against picture, on a borrowed format.** A phone's volume slider sits at the top of the frame.
  Every caption drags it further down, and the picture gets louder: the plan lights up on the field behind the houses,
  one generator at a time, until the slider reads mute and all 76 are humming.
- **Look:** a snowy village street at night with lit windows; the planned hall and generators drawn on the field in
  lines of light, because it is a plan and not yet a building.
- **Beats (about 24 s):**
  1. A quiet village. The slider is at half.
  2. The emails: the town asked its PR agency for help keeping it quiet (Aamuposti). The slider goes down.
  3. The plan lights up: the hall, 96 MW, then 76 generators, one by one.
  4. "The councilors did not know." Windows light up along the street.
  5. €19.2M, Blackstone funds; the complaint to the Chancellor of Justice.
  6. The town: "Nothing was hidden." The slider is at mute and the hum is at its loudest. Cut to silence.
- **Sound:** its own kit: volume ticks, a hum that stacks 76 times, and the cut to nothing.
- **Re-read before a build:** the Aamuposti article itself for the emails' wording and dates (it is behind a paywall),
  the date of the complaint, and any coverage of the 10 Nov residents' meeting. (12 Nov check: the emails, 96 MW,
  16.7 ha and the complaint hold; the 76 generators and €19.2M were not repeated in that day's summaries, so both
  are read at their sources before a build.) It sits near Trade Secret's subject;
  the device and the look are new.
