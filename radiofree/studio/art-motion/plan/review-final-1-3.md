## 1 · Paid For By: FIX (one check)

**The captions in order:**
1. Rival groups aligned with OpenAI and Anthropic spent **$55.7 million**.
2. The New York Times reviewed all **95** ads.
3. They're about everything **except AI**.
4. Only **7** of the 95 mention AI.
5. Voters are souring on AI. The ads **leave it out**.
6. Paid for by AI, which asked not to be **mentioned**.

**Story.** It reads clearly with no confusing beat:
- **Shot 1:** a living room, a campaign ad on the TV, and magenta money falling.
- **Shot 2:** the TV becomes a wall of 95 screens, each ticked.
- **Shot 3:** four topic cards land one at a time.
- **Shot 4:** a highlighter finds seven AI screens, and they step out and count off.
- **Shot 5:** a magenta glove types HE_LTH C_RE / _MM_GR_T_ON. The A and I keys are buried
  under bricks of cash. This is the best image in the three films.

**The joke.** It lands:
- The PAID FOR BY strip is set up in shot 1 and is legible at full size.
- In shot 6 it shrinks to a dot, rolls off the TV and ends as a lump under the rug.
- That lump adds a "swept under the rug" gag on top of the caption.

**Pre-production fixes:**
- **Applied:**
  - The single opening caption, with the US MIDTERMS · NOV 3 tag.
  - NBC's "everything except AI" line.
  - Cards shown one at a time. Their topics (immigration, health care, the President, "and
    more") match NBC's own list.
  - Data centres cut from the caption and the picture.
  - The NYT ANALYSIS · 30.9.2026 tag.
  - The gloved hand, with a cash band on both A and I.
  - The shortened joke.
- **Not applied:** the review's condition, *"Hold until '7 of 95' is confirmed"*.
  - Nothing in the plan or build notes records a check.
  - The research pack still says the NYT original was not reached.
  - Today the NYT, Inquirer and Outpost pages could not be fetched from this sandbox.
  - Search snippets repeat "seven", but only from secondary copy.

**Fairness and naming.**
- Both companies are named in one line, there are no logos, and the money is the same
  colour for both. The two networks are treated alike.
- No real person is drawn and no quote is used.

**Picture.** Nothing is clipped, nothing overlaps the captions, and everything is readable
at 390 px.

**Fixes:**
1. **Blocking.** Read "only seven" in the NYT or Inquirer text before posting.
   - If it holds, ship this file as it is.
   - If it does not hold, change caption 4 to `Few of the 95 · mention AI.`
   - Then re-cut shot 4 so it no longer counts off exactly seven (drop the seven-step
     count-off), and re-render.
2. **Recommended, not blocking.** Recolour the viewer's **red baseball cap** in shot 1, to
   navy or no cap at all.
   - On a US campaign-ad viewer, a red cap reads as MAGA.
   - That casts the targeted voter as one party, and the sources do not support it: the ads
     ran in both parties' primaries.
   - Do this if shot 1 is re-rendered for any other reason.
3. **Optional.** The rolling dot is about 20 px at 1080 and easy to lose on a phone before
   it becomes the lump. Making it about 1.5× bigger would help.

## 2 · On the Same Voluntary Terms: SHIP

**The captions in order:**
1. Britain's AI Security Institute tests new AI **before** it is released.
2. Only when **invited**. No law makes the labs say yes.
3. The White House asked two US labs to **hold back** new models from the UK.
4. The hold lasted **17 days**.
5. Then access came back, on the same **voluntary** terms.
6. Testing has resumed. Until the phone **rings again**.

**Story.** It reads clearly:
- **Shot 1:** a London street with the Elizabeth Tower, a pillar box and a green door with
  a brass AI SECURITY INSTITUTE plate. PRE-RELEASE boxes are carried in.
- **Shot 2:** masked testers have tea beside boxes tagged BY INVITATION.
- **Shot 3:** a red phone rings on the far shore. That shore has the labs, the White House
  and the Washington Monument. Two of the three boxes are reeled away on strings pulled
  from that end.
- **Shot 4:** a DAY 1 → **17** calendar tears off.
- **Shot 5:** the boxes return on the same strings.

**The joke.** It lands:
- The OPEN · BY INVITATION card is hung, and the camera pulls back to the far shore.
- The phone rattles, and the door swings shut under the card at about 37.1 s, just before
  the fade.
- The caption and the picture make one joke.

**Pre-production fixes:** all applied.
- Captions 1–6 as specified.
- The quote marks dropped from "same voluntary terms".
- All three tags carry their dates.
- The London and Washington landmarks.
- The phone and the strings at the labs' end.
- Two of the three boxes reeled away.
- A big 17.
- The callback ending with the door shutting.

**Fairness and naming.**
- No lab is named on screen ("two US labs"), and no person is named.
- The testers wear Toko masks.
- There are no quotes.

**Picture.** Nothing is clipped. The tower spire passes behind caption 1 but stays legible.
At phone size the reeled-away boxes in shot 3 are small (about 10 px). The caption carries
the beat, so this is not a fix.

## 3 · The Newsreader Was Fake: SHIP

**The captions in order:**
1. Finnish newsreaders found their faces in **scam ads**.
2. In April, they asked Meta to **remove** the fakes.
3. Meta sells its ad space by **auction**.
4. Reuters: in Meta's papers, suspected scammers were charged **more**.
5. A German court: Meta picks who sees each ad, so it is **liable**.
6. The newsreader was fake. The **invoice** was real.

**Story.** It reads clearly, and each picture carries its own beat:
- **Shot 1:** a phone at a snowy Helsinki window shows a sponsored post. The cardboard
  newsreader has a blank oval head and holds GET RICH WITH CRYPTO.
- **Shot 2:** faceless front-on silhouettes hold THAT'S NOT ME cards.
- **Shot 3:** an auction. The crook-mask paddle wins, the slot is stamped SOLD and turns
  into the scam ad.
- **Shot 4:** a ledger reads SUSPECTED SCAMMER → PRICE ↑, with a readable META: A DETERRENT
  card.
- **Shot 5:** a robed judge in silhouette sits before a Frankfurt skyline, behind an URTEIL
  headed LANDGERICHT FRANKFURT AM MAIN and stamped LIABLE in magenta.

**The joke.** It lands:
- The cutout topples, and the GET RICH sign drops.
- Behind it, a cash register prints an INVOICE receipt that runs out of the frame.
- The joke is set up by SOLD in shot 3 and PRICE ↑ in shot 4.

**Pre-production fixes:** all applied.
- Single captions 2, 4 and 5, and the kept ending.
- The REUTERS · NOV 2025 tag.
- **FRANKFURT REGIONAL COURT · 16.9.2026 · CAN BE APPEALED.** "Can be appealed" is said,
  and it is readable at 390 px.
- Meta's side is shown in the deterrent card.
- No broadcaster logos on the scam ad; the desk is a generic NEWS desk. YLE · MTV UUTISET
  appears only as the source tag.
- No gavel in the court scene.
- INVOICE on the paper.

**Fairness and naming.**
- No real newsreader is drawn or imitated.
- The Frankfurt plaintiff is not named.
- There are no quotes.
- The internal documents are attributed to Reuters.

**Picture.** Nothing is clipped and nothing overlaps the captions. The cutout falls on its
back rather than on its face, which still reads.
