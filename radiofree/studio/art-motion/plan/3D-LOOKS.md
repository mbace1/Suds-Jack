# RFH 3D: a family of looks, not one truck kit (owner, 6 Nov)

**Owner, 6 Nov:** "You can plan and make other 3d than trucks. Yeah the Muhos 3d one was a bigger success than others but
let's not base it on the trucks solely."

Cut First (Doodle 8) did better than the papercut films. We don't yet know whether the art, the topic or both made the
difference: Unmarked Boxes tests the art and Five Pins the topic. The trucks are one subject in that look, not the look
itself.

## What we keep from Cut First (the look)

- A **toy-world diorama**: low-poly, rounded, matte, seen at a slight tilt-shift scale, with a soft anime grade (warm
  key, sky-blue fill, film grain) from `looks/cars/`.
- **One clear physical action per beat.** Something falls, stops, tears or lands on the word that pops.
- **Signs and props carry the facts**: a STOP sign, a calendar, a tag. There is no data chart.
- **A slow rise at the end** that shows the whole place, then the RFH sign-off.
- **Rules:** the house rules apply. US spelling, Google named plainly, no real faces, and sources carried on tags.
