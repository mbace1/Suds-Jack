# Review: "You Looked At It" v2 (成片_rfp_v2.mp4)

**Verdict: 8/10, SHIP.** There are no RED items. Both v1 RED items and seven of the eight YELLOW items are fixed. What remains is polish.

Specs: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 15.00 s, AAC 48 kHz. Loudness -14.0 LUFS, true peak -2.7 dBTP. Cuts land at 3.233, 6.467, 9.667 and 12.900 s, within 1.3 frames of the grid. The sound events fall at 0.55 and 0.85, 1.51, 2.10, 4.28, 5.35, the stamps at 6.96–9.10, 10.71, 11.78, 13.82 and 14.20, all where the brief puts them.

## Problems

### RED (must fix)

None.

### YELLOW (should fix)

1. **1.7–3.2 s: the push-in crops the location sign.** The "S" of SENAATINTORI goes behind the left edge of the frame, with only about 60% of it visible at 2.9 s. This is the word that says Helsinki. **Fix:** move the push anchor about 80 px right, or keep the sign at least 30 px inside the frame.
2. **3.23–6.4 s: the shot 2 joke chain is weaker than in v1.**
   - "ALSO ORDERED?" (5.36 s) settles on the top-left of the glass, pointing at nothing.
   - With the lens's birch leaf gone, "BIRCH LEAF ?" on the receipt has no setup.
   - The target cake is split by the window bar, and its own ORDERED label covers its top tier.
   - **Fix:** clip ALSO ORDERED? onto a birch leaf falling past the glass. Move the target cake clear of the bar. Lift the label above the cake.
3. **3.23–6.4 s: shot 2 is murky and static.** The back of the head is a near-black disc on near-black brick, and the strap is a dark line on dark. The camera is locked: about 1% of pixels change over 4.5–5.0 and 5.5–6.4 s. **Fix:** add an ochre rim from the window light along the head and strap, and a slow 2–3% push or drift.

6. 9.13 s: the TOTAL rule still runs through the top of "$249".

8. Leaves still cross text: on the wedge (2.2–2.9 s), on the shot 2 labels (5.4–6.3 s), on the billboard's "L" (11.85 s) and on the "O" of LOOKING (15.0 s).

10. 13.2–14.0 s: the forearm card overlapping the upper arm ends in a flat cut. It reads as a puppet joint and is acceptable.
