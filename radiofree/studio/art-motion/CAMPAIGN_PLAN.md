## 2. Messaging pillars (Middler writes the final copy; these lines show direction only)
Format: one sharp line, then a question, then 2–4 hashtags. The same copy goes on IG and X.
1. **Who decides?** (RFH, every series): power hidden in process. *"Britain tests new AI before release, but only if the companies agree. Who decides when the testers get a look? #AIsafety #AI"* (Coxon 4)
2. **Count to 3 Nov** (Coxon): the midterms as the clock. *"Five AI companies invited. Three came only under threat of subpoena. None gave the odds. Midterms 3 Nov. #midterms #AI"* (Coxon 2)
3. **Local insight, global outlook** (Nordic and Doodle): Nordic news that matters outside the Nordics. *"The regulator said stop by 23.10. Who's waiting on whom? #Muhos #datakeskus #Finland"*
4. **Who's really in the box?** (Toko): the person or gap behind the gadget. *"Batteries included. Parents not included. 🤖"* (already approved) / *"Somebody always did the dishes. Now he's just further away."* (draft on file)
5. **The brink** (new strand, owner 19 Oct): *"Radio Free Helsinki is also about the dystopian future, trying to see how espionage and surveillance is almost at a brink of war in Finland and similar spots."* Military tech, espionage, surveillance and hybrid pressure on the front lines (Finland, the Baltics, Poland, Norway's north, the Baltic Sea, and frontier places like it). Sober, never war hype, no tactical detail; aim at secrecy, surveillance creep and the procurement machine. Research: `plan/research-brink-next.md`.
6. **Global tech** (new strand, owner 19 Oct): tech news from beyond the US, Nordic and Doodle strands, read as the dystopian near future: the quiet step toward a world nobody voted for. Never aimed at a nation or a people. Research: `plan/research-global-next.md`.
