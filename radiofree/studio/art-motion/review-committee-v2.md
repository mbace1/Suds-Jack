# Review: "Committee of the Whole" v2 (cw_v2_captions.mp4)

**Score: 7/10. Verdict: ship with fixes.** The sourcing is solid and the house rules on real people are kept. One caption gives a remark the wrong scope, and one quote loses a qualifier that matters. Both are cheap to fix. The picture is clean, but it goes slack twice.

## Findings, by severity

1. **MAJOR. 26.5–31.8, caption 5 and the FLIPPANT stamp.** City & State, AP and NY1 all report that Menin said "flippant at best" to OpenAI's answer: Morgan Dwyer said she didn't know the odds and the exact number didn't matter. Caption 4 ends on "none of the four companies", so "that answer" reads as all four. That puts her rebuke on Meta, Doodle and Anthropic. **Fix:** "The Speaker called OpenAI's answer / 'flippant at best.'" In the picture, either land the stamp on OPENAI's "?" card or carry the OPENAI plate into the gavel shot.

2. **MAJOR. 15.9–18.9, Coxon's quote, plus the dial at 15.0–19.3.** The sourced line is "On our current path, I think it is more likely than not that humanity loses control…". The caption drops "on our current path" and "I think". That turns a conditional, personal estimate into a flat prediction, which edges into misstating his view. The dial hangs on the council's own wall with the needle at about 58, so it reads as an official reading of a number he never gave. **Fix:** keep the qualifier outside the quote marks: *Jacob Coxon, ex-Anthropic: on the current path, it is "more likely than not that humanity loses control to these AIs."* Or quote AP's full sentence over 4–5 lines. Then label the dial face "> 50%", or "COXON · ON THE CURRENT PATH", as the shot table planned. That label is not drawn now.

3. **MEDIUM. 0.8–6.1, caption 1 and the banner.** "Sat all 51 members" claims full attendance, and no search found a roll call. A Committee of the Whole means all 51 are members of it, not that all 51 sat. "Sat" used as a transitive verb also reads awkwardly. **Fix:** "On 5 October, New York's city council met as a committee of all 51 members / for one hearing: AI risk." The banner's "ALL 51 MEMBERS" is fine because it describes the committee. But "5.10.2026" reads as 10 May to a US audience on a New York story. Change it to "OCT 5 2026".

4. **MEDIUM. 26.0–28.9, dead stretch.** The gavel hovers on a frame that is about 60% bare wall. The score drops to clock ticks (−37 to −43 dB RMS for about 3 s). "The Speaker called that answer" sits alone with no payoff for 2.2 s. **Fix:** bring the stamp in around 27.3, or cut about 1.2 s from the shot. Better still, frame the masked Speaker's hand and the gavel larger. A smaller version of the same lull: 2.0–6.4, where nothing happens after the banner lands except falling leaves.

5. **MEDIUM-LOW. 13.9–19.3, in-picture text.** The microphone head covers the K in "THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF NEW YORK", so it reads "NEW YOR". **Fix:** move the mic stem right or lower, or start the text further left.

6. **LOW. 20.0–25.7.** The wire that "THE ODDS?" hangs from runs straight down x=540, through all four caption lines. **Fix:** hang the card from the rail below the caption block, or fade the wire out above y≈600.

7. **LOW. 19.3–25.7.** The ANTHROPIC plate is set smaller than META, DOODLE and OPENAI. It looks like an auto-fit bug, and it is the one plate you can't read on a phone. **Fix:** give all four plates the same width and point size. Also, the far-right mic head touches the frame edge.

8. **LOW. Craft.**
   - The cuts at 6.4–6.9 and 12.9–13.4 are muddy double exposures: the SUBPOENA stamp over the dial, the masks over the envelopes. Use hard cuts on the beat.
   - The gavel head is drawn end-on while its handle is in profile, so it reads as a barrel.
   - At phone size, Coxon's white nape band reads as a clerical collar, a "doom preacher" connotation nobody intended. Draw an ordinary shirt collar.

9. **LOW. 34.3–37.2.** "The council is taking it to court" is supported by the hearing statement ("we are pursuing that subpoena in court"). As of 13 Oct no search found a filing. That's acceptable. The safer wording is "The council says it will enforce it in court." The TO COURT stamp can stay.

## Checked and passing

  - The envelope order also matches the reporting: Doodle and OpenAI said yes on Sunday, and Anthropic late Sunday night, hours before the subpoenas were to be served.
  - Showing OpenAI etc. as YES after the stamp is fair: the stamp lands above the letters rather than on them, and the caption says "threat".
  - SpaceXAI blank is fair, since it never responded.
- **"None of the four companies gave a number."** Supported by AP, NY1 and City & State. The blank "?" cards are fair.
- **SpaceXAI absent despite the 28 Sept subpoena.** Supported by the council's press release and the hearing statement.
- **Coxon's views.** Nothing implies he opposes interim self-regulation by the labs: no bills or regulation framing sits near him. Keep it that way, and don't add any line suggesting he backs the city bills. He has said proposed legislation would quickly go obsolete.
- **Real-person rules.** Coxon is shown only from behind, the council wear Toko masks, and Google is Doodle. All pass.
- **Captions.** The widest line is about 720 px. None is clipped. All sit top-anchored at y≈270, clear of the key detail apart from finding 6. Keywords pop in sync with their picture events: the stamp at 29.2, the gavel at 30.0, TO COURT at 35.25.
- **Audio.** −14.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.9 dBFS, LRA 8.8 LU. At the end, the horn after TO COURT rings to about 36.4 s, then fades with the picture to silence at 37.5. It rings out rather than being chopped.
