# Review: "You Looked At It" (成片_rfp.mp4)

**Verdict: 7/10, SHIP WITH FIXES.** Every beat is on screen and on the grid, and the papercut shadowbox holds across all five shots. Two body-continuity errors must be fixed first.

Specs: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 15.00 s, AAC 48 kHz. Loudness -14.0 LUFS, true peak -2.7 dBTP. Cuts at 3.233, 6.467, 9.667 and 12.900 s, each within 1.3 frames of the grid.

## Problems

### RED (must fix)

1. **6.47–9.1 s, shot 3: floating forearm.** The sleeve under the receipt-holding hand ends in mid-frame with a flat, shadowed bottom edge (y≈1040 at 6.5 s, y≈1635 at 8.3 s; the frame bottom is 1860). It reads as a cut-off limb for about 2.5 s. **Fix:** anchor the sleeve to the bottom edge of the frame and stretch it as the receipt grows.
2. **12.9–15.0 s, shot 5: headless coat.** Both shoulders and the scarf loop are in frame, and the skyline shows through the empty neck: no mask, no chin. The final image is an empty coat. From 13.2–14.1 s the sleeve also has a flat-cut top end on the chest. **Fix:** crop so the collar leaves the top of the frame, or show the bottom of the mask disc. Run the sleeve off the frame.

### YELLOW (should fix)

3. **14.66–14.86 s: the last hit is gated, not rung out.** The 14.20 s hit holds flat at -13 dB, then drops 30 dB in about 180 ms (22 dB of it in 80 ms). The last frame is silent, but on a phone it sounds like the audio stops, just as the second glow fires. **Fix:** give it a natural exponential or reverb decay from 14.2 s, reaching about -40 dB by 14.95 s.
4. **3.23–6.4 s, shot 2: no lock-on, mostly static.** The lens mask clips the AR corners on the cake; only a 10 px tick shows. Only 0.6–1.7% of pixels change over 3.5–4.25, 4.5–5.3 and 5.5–6.4 s, and there is no drift. The lower half is empty until 4.30 s, and the lower left stays empty. **Fix:** draw the brackets inside the lens and snap them in at about 3.7 s. Add lens-versus-mask parallax and a bottom-left element.
5. **4.4–6.4 s: the right frame edge clips the last "D" of ORDERED.** Shift the wedge 60 px left.
6. **10.0–12.9 s, shot 4: the leaf and its string cover the billboard joke.** The falling leaf crosses "K", "E" and "FIRST" while the viewer is reading, and the string then crosses "FREE" and "FIRST". Route the fall beside the billboard and hang the string from the shelter roof.
7. **11.8 s: "all LEDs light" does not read.** The LEDs are about 15 px (about 6 px on a phone). Yellow and pink are already lit at 9.7 s, and two go dark again by 12.7 s. Keep all four dark until 11.78 s, then light them together with a bigger pulse and keep them lit.
8. **0–3.2 s: the walker's arm is upside down.** The sleeve hangs from the cuff past the coat hem, and the open palm reads as a wave, not a grip. Run the forearm up to the shoulder and close the fingers on the shaft.
9. **Shot 1: early dark is half sold.** The lamps and the 4:02 clock say "early", but the bright orange-yellow horizon says golden hour. Take the horizon down a stop toward dusk violet, and make the lamps the brightest thing in the frame.

### GREEN (nitpicks)

10. 0.60–0.75 s: the wedge's pop overshoot clips at the left inner frame edge.
11. 9.13 s: the TOTAL rule runs through the top of "$249".
12. 14.25–15.0 s: leaves cross the "S" and the "O" of STILL LOOKING.
13. Shot 4 does not clearly read as a tram stop. The head turn is a disc rotating, so it reads as a tilt.
14. The shot 2 lenses are tall ovals while the glasses in shot 1 are round. The cathedral has no pediment.
