v2 (owner, 2026-10-13): "people are starting to revolt — more on the angle of no vote from the people, nor confidence from locals". ## Sources (the naming rule for films: README, "Naming in the films")

Doodle is Google; Tuike Finland Oy is its Finnish company. Places and the regulator are real.

- Announcement, 9 Sept 2026: at least €13 bn over 2027–2028 in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala, with "dedicated nature and community funds to support local biodiversity, education, research, and workforce development". Google Cloud press release; Euronews; Helsinki Times; TelecomTV.
- Halt order, 7 Oct 2026: the supervisory agency LVV ordered Tuike Finland to suspend, by 23 October, all preparatory measures that would significantly alter the environment at Muhos and Kajaani, with a written explanation due by 14 October. Al Jazeera; All Things Nordic; The Next Web.
- About 330 ha cleared at Muhos (a figure reported as Tuike's own) and just under 200 ha at Kajaani; Finnish law requires an EIA before permanent clear-cutting over 200 ha. Martin Cid; The Next Web; byteiota.
- Response: Google said it "fell short of our own high standards" and plans tree planting across 130 ha at Muhos. Capital FM / AFP.
- Council: Muhos municipal council approved the zoning plan for the site (Pyhänsivu–Leppiniemi) in early March 2026, under a land-use agreement worth €26 m to the municipality; one private individual appealed it to the administrative court, delaying the payment. Yle.
- Protest: about 100 people protested outside Google's Helsinki office on Arkadiankatu on 12 Oct 2026, organised by Luontoliitto. Helsinki Times; Yle.
- Initiative: the citizens' initiative "Datakeskukset kuriin" (limits on data centres' grid connections and a data-centre fee), opened 15 Sept 2026, passed the 50,000 signatures needed for parliament within a few days. Kaleva.
- Local opinion in Muhos is reported as mixed (jobs and tax revenue against doubts). Kaleva; Yle. The film therefore shows the residents unasked, not opposed.
