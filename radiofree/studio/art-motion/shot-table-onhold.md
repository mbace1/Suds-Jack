## Sources (the naming rule for films: README, "Naming in the films")

Doodle is Google (Alphabet). Places, the tax and the government are real.

- The hold, Oct 2025: Alphabet told the Finnish government its Kajaani and Muhos plans were on hold until the electricity-tax level was known; the proposal then was to raise data centres' rate from 0.05 to 2.19 c/kWh. Google's consultation statement: it had "not yet made an investment decision regarding the Kajaani and Muhos sites". Yle (yle.fi/a/74-20189966, after HS); DatacenterDynamics, "Plans on hold for Google data center in Finland following potential electricity tax raise"; Kaleva/HS; Verde, 23.10.2025.
- The tax, 1 July 2026: data centres moved out of electricity-tax class II (0.05 c/kWh) into class I (2.24 c/kWh), adopted Dec 2025; the government estimated +€47 m a year in revenue. Valtioneuvosto, "Government safeguards Finland's competitiveness in attracting data centre investments"; IEA policy database.
- 2.24 / 0.05 = 44.8: "about 45 times" is arithmetic on the two official rates.
- The dropped support, 1 Sept 2026: after the 2027 budget talks the government dropped the planned replacement support (about €30 m a year); Finance Minister Riikka Purra said Finland's attractiveness did not depend on tax subsidies (paraphrase, never in quote marks). Xinhua via The Star, "Finland drops planned financial support for data centers", 2.9.2026 (figures via Yle); MTV Uutiset; Verkkouutiset.
- The announcement, 9 Sept 2026: at least €13 bn over 2027–2028 in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala. Google blog, "Google's AI commitment to Finland"; AFP; Helsinki Times.
- The second hold, 6 Oct 2026: the Permit and Supervision Agency (LVV) told Tuike Finland Oy, Google's Finnish company, to suspend immediately, and by 23.10.2026 at the latest, the preparatory works at Muhos and Kajaani (felling, earthworks, site roads) until the environmental impact assessment is done; written explanation by 14.10. LVV press release via STT Info, 6.10.2026 (sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72390663); Kaleva; Verkkouutiset; Al Jazeera/AFP 7.10.2026. "Told" because the instrument is a kehotus (a call to suspend), backed by possible administrative coercion.
- The support was a cap: "up to" €30 m a year (Valtioneuvosto 5.12.2025, ministerial committee on economic policy).
- The cheque's payee is Doodle's own data centres: the €13 bn is capital spending on its own sites, not money paid to Finland (review v2).
