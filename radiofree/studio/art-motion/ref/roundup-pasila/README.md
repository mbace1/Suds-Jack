# Roundup in front of the Yle campus, Pasila (owner's photos, Sat 7 Nov 2026)

Five photos by the owner: a 5 L **Roundup Piha** jug ("rikkakasvien torjunta-aine", weed-control product, glyphosate)
standing in a flowerbed among nettles and fallen leaves, with the Yle sign and the Mediatalo / Luova talo / Studiotalo
signpost behind it, and under a tree by the rock. The jug is weathered and spattered. Label claims visible: "Tehoaa
jopa vuorokaudessa", "Mahdollisuus jopa 10 minuutin yhtäjaksoiseen ruiskutukseen", "Hävittää rikkakasvit juurineen",
"Näkyviä tuloksia 24 tunnissa", "Tehoaa kortteita lukuun ottamatta lähes kaikkeen kasvillisuuteen".

**What the photos prove:** a Roundup jug was there on 7 Nov. **What they do not prove:** who left it, whether it was
used there, whether it is empty, or that Yle, the city or any contractor sprayed anything. Any film says "found", not
"used". Candidate use: a Finnish coda or photo film beside On the Label (Coxon 14). Rights: the owner's own photos.
