# RFH lattice-mast icon: v3 variations

These build on `icon-c2-lattice-mast-v2`. Every variation keeps a traditional tapering lattice mast, broadcast waves and the RFH palette, with no text and no Toko mask. Each has a `-1080.png` square and a `-circle.png` crop. `contact-sheet.png` shows each one at 400px, 64px and 48px. Regenerate with `python3 make_mast_v3.py [v3-N ...]`.

- **v3-1 night-moon-rooftops**: a cream mast on a violet-to-night gradient sky with a crescent moon, magenta arcs, and two layers of papercut Helsinki rooftops (with the cathedral) at the base.
- **v3-2 ochre-on-magenta**: an ochre mast on a magenta disc, with deep plum stacked paper wave arcs and a cream tip light. The colour is the lead.
- **v3-3 magenta-mast-ochre-ripples**: a heavy magenta mast on night, with full ochre ripple rings spreading from a glowing tip.
- **v3-4 stacked-paper-arcs**: a cream mast in front of alternating magenta and ochre paper arc bands, each lifted on its own shadow, over low rooftops.
- **v3-5 duotone-ochre-dusk**: a night-silhouette mast, cathedral and rooftops against an ochre dusk sky, with night waves. Magenta appears only on the tip glow.
- **v3-6 bold-emblem**: a heavy three-bay mast set low, with thick magenta side waves `((( )))` and an ochre ring. This is the strongest at 48px.
