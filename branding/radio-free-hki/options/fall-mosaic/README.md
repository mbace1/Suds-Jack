# Radio Free Helsinki: fall-colour paper mosaic icons

Final icon pass. Torn and cut paper tiles in autumn colours (ochre #e3a33a, amber, rust, deep red, cream) with night gaps between tiles. Magenta #f0027f appears only as a small accent. No text and no Toko face.

Each icon has a `-1080.png` square and a `-circle.png` crop. `contact-sheet.png` shows each one at 400, 64 and 48px. Regenerate with `python3 make_fall_mosaic.py [m1 ...]`.

- **m1 mast-mosaic**: a communications mast silhouette built from fall mosaic tiles, with a magenta tip accent.
- **m2 skyline-mosaic**: a Helsinki skyline and cathedral dome built from mosaic tiles across the lower half of the disc.
- **m3 abstract-disc**: a full-circle abstract field of torn autumn paper tiles, with sparse magenta accents.
- **m4 cathedral-mast-mosaic**: cathedral + radio mast skyline in mosaic, magenta tip on the mast.
- **m5 wreath-mast**: a mosaic mast in the centre, ringed by a wreath of larger floating paper scraps (one magenta).

**Small sizes:** m3 and m5 stay the strongest at 48px as shapes. m1 and m4 keep a readable mast/cathedral silhouette down to 64px.
