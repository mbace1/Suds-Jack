# Art sheet request: WORLD 3 — SEWER MODULES

For Grok (owner, 2026-10-31). Attach `world-3-sewer-reference.png`: on its left
is the approved World 2 sheet, the format to match; on its right is World 3
as the game shows it now, for the camera and the layout. Once the owner
approves a sheet, Claude builds it the way World 2 was built (v65-v66).

---

Make ONE environment art sheet in exactly the style and layout of the approved
"World 2 — Curb / Lawn Modules" sheet: white background, watercolour with clean
ink lines, 8 numbered modules with hand-lettered labels, each with its size in
metres. Title: "Toko Drop 2.0 environment art sheet / WORLD 3 — SEWER MODULES".
Mark it PENDING APPROVAL.

The world: the hero (a 1 m white gel blob) has dropped through the driveway's
storm drain into the sewer below. The arena is a long tunnel, 60 × 22 m, seen
from above at about 30°, as in World 2. It is daytime up top, so sunlight falls
in through street grates in shafts; the rest is cool, damp and teal. Worms
(WORMA, and the boss MAW, a great pink worm) swim under the floor and come up
through it.

Rules for every module:
- Micro Machines scale: the 1 m hero is small next to them. Draw the hero
  beside module 1 for scale.
- Simple solid shapes a 3D artist can build from boxes, cylinders and bevels.
  No fine detail that vanishes from the camera.
- The floor stays mid-dark and quiet, so the bright jelly enemies read on it.
- Nothing tall along the near side: walls at most 1.2 m.
- No characters, no letters, no creatures, no spectators.

The 8 modules:
1. Straight brick wall segment: 3 m long, 1.2 m tall, 0.6 m thick, a stone
   capstone on top, damp at the bottom, a seam between segments.
2. Wall corner pier: a square brick pier where a side wall meets a tunnel end.
3. Iron bar gate segment: closes each tunnel end; vertical bars in a frame,
   darkness behind.
4. Wall pipe outlet: a round pipe mouth set in the wall, a trickle of green
   water running out; one every 10 m or so.
5. Wet flagstone floor: running bond, 1.7 × 0.85 m slabs, dark grout, a few
   shallow puddles.
6. Water channel: 2.4 m wide down the middle of the tunnel, flowing water
   between two stone kerbs; arrows for the flow.
7. Sunlight from a street grate: the pool of light a grate above throws on
   the floor, with its bar pattern.
8. Arena layout (guide only): the tunnel from above at 30°, walls on both long
   sides, gates at both ends, the channel down the middle.
