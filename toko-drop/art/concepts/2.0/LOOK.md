# Toko Drop 2.0 — Look for Claude (meshes / in-game)

**Read this before meshing.** Concept watercolor sheets are for shape/layout only.

## What the game should look like NOW

- **Not** cel-shaded.
- **Not** watercolor as the in-game shader (watercolor = mood / concept paper only).
- **Production target:** translucent **jelly / gel** materials + **simple Wind Waker–type readability** (clear silhouettes, soft lighting, readable forms) so lots of VFX and campaign themes (Surface → Kiln) still work.
- Soft clearcoat / transmission on gels; curb/lawn/concrete = matte readable grey-green summer driveway, not anime ink outlines.

## What concept sheets are for

- Module shape, proportions, which pieces exist.
- Camera ~30° iso (match approved curb sheet).
- Scale: Micro Machines (tiny hero vs curb).
- Do **not** copy watercolor washes, paper texture, or hand-ink linework into the mesh materials.

## Hero / enemies (when approved)

- Hero: white soft gel dome/blob.
- Eyes: elongated black ovals + small white highlight (concept only until hero sheet APPROVED).
- Cubes: soft jelly, no letters.
- **Not** Midori Edition / 4.0 Drive kit.
