A city pigeon, seen from the side facing right, standing. Blue-grey body, darker wing bars, an oil-slick green and purple sheen on the neck, orange-red eye ring, a small pale bill. ONE FOOT IS A HEALED STUMP, the way half the pigeons in a city are, and it stands on the good one. Feathers slightly scruffy and city-dirty. Small, calm and completely unbothered.
