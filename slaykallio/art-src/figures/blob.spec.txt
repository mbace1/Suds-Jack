A chest-high mutating blob of acid yellow-green ooze, seen from the side. Sagging under its own weight, wider at the bottom, with a rippling top edge. Rubbish it has swallowed is half-visible suspended inside it: a glass bottle, a crushed drinks can, a bent bicycle spoke. Along its upper body, bulges that are on their way to becoming arms and are not there yet. A couple of mismatched dark eyes near the top. Municipal green going acid and sour.
