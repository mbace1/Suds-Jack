An enormous park bear STATUE that has started walking, seen from the side facing right, on all fours, head turned slightly toward the viewer. Its whole body is weathered cast bronze gone grey-green, or dark granite — a hard mineral surface, not fur, with the tool marks and casting seams still in it and moss packed into the seams and under the jaw. One shoulder is chipped pale where something has knocked it. The paws are flat-bottomed, as if they were part of a plinth. Its EYES are the only warm thing on it and they glow a dull amber from inside the stone. It is colossal — far larger than any animal beside it.
