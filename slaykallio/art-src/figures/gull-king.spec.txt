A herring gull the size of a large dog, seen from the side facing right, wings spread wide and high. Scarred and chipped yellow bill, one eye clouded white and blind, plumage filthy and stained brown along the belly and wing edges. On its head, wedged down over the crown, a CIRCLET MADE OF RUBBISH — a bent bottle-cap ring and a twist of wire, something it found rather than anything made for it. It must read as a gull first and a king second.
