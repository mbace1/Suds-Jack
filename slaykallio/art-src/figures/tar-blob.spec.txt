A chest-high blob of ROAD TAR, seen from the side. Near black and glossy, with a hardened cracked crust over its upper surface like cooled asphalt, warmer dark brown showing through the cracks. Heavy and armoured-looking rather than wet — it can plate itself over. Sagging base stuck to the ground, a few stiff strings of tar pulling up from its edges. One or two dull eyes set deep in the black.
