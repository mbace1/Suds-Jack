A rat the size of a large dog, the king of the sewer, seen from the side facing right. Hunched up on its haunches so the silhouette has a real SHOULDER and a curved spine, forepaws off the ground. Matted fur going grey at the muzzle, old scars, a tail as thick as a rope. Behind and below it, a tangle of knotted rat tails and rusted bin-wire snarled together on the ground — the folklore rat-king knot, which is what makes him a king. He wears NO crown and no clothing of any kind.
