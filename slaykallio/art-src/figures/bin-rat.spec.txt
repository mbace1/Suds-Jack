The same species of brown canal rat, grown wrong on refuse: half again as large, heavier through the shoulders, seen from the side facing right. Coarse patchy fur with a bald scarred flank, one ear torn away, incisors too long for the jaw and stained yellow. Standing squarer on its feet than an ordinary rat, head up. It must read unmistakably as the same animal as a common rat, only bigger and sicker — not a different creature.
